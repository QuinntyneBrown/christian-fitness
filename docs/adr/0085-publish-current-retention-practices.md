# 0085. Publish the retention practices in operation

## Status

Accepted, 25 November 2026.

## Context

L2-067 AC8 requires accurate public statements. AC9 describes the retention periods in ADR 0078. Journey 36 delivers the scheduled erasure and retention jobs.

## Decision

Publish and link both documents now. Describe the records currently held and direct members to the privacy contact for access and deletion requests. Journey 36 must verify the deployed jobs and their schedules before publishing the periods, shared-message treatment and audit rules in a new version under ADR 0063. Apply the same rule to the summary and Terms.

## Options considered

Delaying publication would leave people without either document at Join. Publishing the eventual periods now would promise a deadline the service cannot meet.

## Consequences

Members can read the current policy before joining. AC9 remains owned by journey 36 until its retention rules operate in production.
