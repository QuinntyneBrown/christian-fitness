Pattern

Forms

Forms ask for as little as possible, say what's wrong in plain words, and never lose what someone typed.

WCAG 2.2 AA

Overview

Every form in Christian Fitness, from sign-in to the admin event editor, follows one shape: a single column of form fields, an error summary that appears at the top only after submit, and a footer whose primary button names the outcome ("Send request", "Publish", "Save").

  • Ask less. Optional fields say (optional); required ones say nothing. Faith details such as home church are always optional and never required to finish a form.
  • Validate on submit. Errors appear when the person presses the primary button, not while they type.
  • Keep everything. A failed submit keeps every value; the message says "Nothing was lost".

When to use

  • Auth screens (sign in, join, forgot and reset password, two-step): a full-width .auth__form with a large block submit.
  • Short edits (edit profile, request a booking): a dialog, which becomes a bottom sheet on phones.
  • Long staff edits (event editor, listing editor): a drawer so the list stays in view.
  • Never an inline form inside a card or row; editing opens a dialog, drawer or screen.

Structure

Top to bottom: title, alert slot (empty until submit), fields in reading order, a quiet hint about what happens next, then the footer with Cancel before the primary action. Fields use .stack; two short related fields (date and time) share a .field-row from the tablet breakpoint.

Responsive behaviour

  • Phones: one column; footer buttons share the width (.btn--grow); dialogs open as bottom sheets with the footer pinned above the keyboard.
  • Tablet and up: .field-row puts two short fields side by side; the auth form sits beside the brand panel.
  • Inputs are 16px so phones don't zoom on focus, and at least 44px tall.

Accessibility

  • Every control has a visible <label for>; placeholders are never the label.
  • Errors set aria-invalid="true" and are joined with aria-describedby; hints are joined the same way when there's no error.
  • The error summary is role="alert", tabindex="-1" and receives focus on submit; each link moves focus to its field.
  • Busy buttons carry aria-busy="true"; the spinner is decorative and the label changes to "Saving…".
  • Enter submits from a single-line field; Esc closes a dialog form only when nothing has changed, otherwise it asks first.
  • Use autocomplete tokens (email, current-password, one-time-code) so password managers and autofill work.

Content

  • Labels are short nouns or questions in sentence case: "Email", "When works for you?".
  • Field errors say how to fix it: "Enter an email address like name@example.com", not "Invalid email".
  • Summary titles count what's left: "2 things to fix before publishing"; a single problem may name it: "Check your email address".
  • The primary button names the outcome; its busy label is the same verb with -ing: "Send request" → "Sending…".
  • See Content and tone for the error message formula.

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • Error summary titles follow three formulas: counted ("2 things to fix before publishing", "1 thing to fix before listing"), "Check your …" (sign in, profile, forgot password) and a bare instruction ("Add when you're free", "Your passwords don't match"). Recommended: counted when there are two or more, "Check your …" for one.
  • Failed alerts mix "We couldn't save your changes" with subjectless "Couldn't publish" and "Couldn't save the listing". Recommended: the "We couldn't …" form in the member app; staff screens may stay terse but should be consistent.
  • Join labels "Home church" without "(optional)"; faith details must be marked optional.
  • Busy submit buttons use disabled plus aria-busy, so focus drops from the button while saving. Prefer keeping it focusable with aria-disabled="true".
