Content and tone

We sound like a friend from church who also coaches: warm, plain, encouraging, and never preachy.

WCAG 2.2 AA

Overview

Christian Fitness is a community for health in body and spirit. Words should welcome people of every fitness level and every church, and never make faith a test of belonging.

  • Warm: "Good morning, Grace", "We've started the conversation for you".
  • Plain: short sentences, everyday words, Canadian English (colour, centre, cheque).
  • Encouraging, not pushy: invite ("Find your next run"), don't guilt ("You missed 3 workouts").
  • Faith, lightly held: scripture appears in the verse band with its citation; prayer and devotion are named plainly where an event includes them ("Stretch & Pray"). No jargon that assumes one tradition.

When to use

These rules apply to every surface: member app, admin console, e-mails and notifications. The admin console may be terser ("Couldn't publish") but stays respectful about members: describe behaviour, not character ("Shared supplement links in 3 chats", not "Spammer").

Structure

The error message formula

What happened (no blame) + what's safe or what it means + what to do. Lead with "We couldn't…" or "[Thing] didn't load", never "You failed to…" or "Error".

We couldn't register you

Your spot isn't saved yet. Check your connection and try again.

What happened: "We couldn't register you". What it means: "Your spot isn't saved yet". What to do: "Check your connection and try again".

States

Tone shifts with the moment:

  • Success: brief and glad. "You're registered."
  • Waiting: the verb, in progress. "Saving…"
  • Empty: hopeful, with a next step. "The first ones are on their way."
  • Error: calm and reassuring. "Nothing was lost."
  • Moderation: neutral and factual. "Kevin can't sign in or message anyone until you lift it."

Responsive behaviour

Copy doesn't change by breakpoint; write the short version first so it fits a phone. Labels stay under three words where possible; long event titles wrap rather than truncate on detail pages and clamp to two lines on cards.

Accessibility

  • Link and button text makes sense alone: "Edit Easter Sunrise 5K", not "Click here".
  • Write dates and times in full where they are announced: "Saturday, April 18 at 6:45 AM".
  • Don't rely on direction or colour: "the red field", "the button on the right".
  • Names, messages and errors from people or the server are rendered as text, never HTML.
  • Aim for grade 7 reading level; avoid idioms that don't translate.

Content

  • Sentence case everywhere: "Request a booking", not "Request A Booking". Proper nouns keep their capitals (Easter Sunrise 5K, Martin Goodman Trail).
  • Numbers as digits: "3 days", "142 going", "6 spots left". Prices "$10", "Free", "from $70".
  • Dates: "Sat Apr 18" on cards, "Saturday, April 18" in sentences; times "6:45 AM".
  • Verbs for actions: Register, Message, Publish, Approve, Decline, Suspend.
  • Contractions are fine and friendly: "You're", "didn't", "We'll".
  • Faith details are always optional; say "(optional)" and never imply they're needed.
