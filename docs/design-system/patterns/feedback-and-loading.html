Pattern

Feedback and loading

Every action gets an answer: right away, in the place the person is looking, and in proportion to what happened.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview
  • When to use
  • Structure
  • States
  • Responsive

Overview

Feedback has three jobs: show that something is happening, confirm what happened, and offer a way back. Pick the lightest element that does the job.

SituationUse
Page or region loading for the first timeSkeleton shaped like the content
A button's action is in flightBusy button: spinner plus "-ing" label
Small region refreshing or loading moreInline spinner with a text label
Long task with known size (upload)Progress bar
Done, and the person may want to undoToast with Undo
Done inside a dialogDialog success panel
FailedInline alert, error state or danger toast (see Notifications)

When to use

  • Skeleton, not spinner, for first loads of anything with a known shape (event grid, profile, tables). It keeps the layout still.
  • Spinner only inside a button or for a small region where a skeleton would be bigger than the result.
  • Nothing for actions under 300 ms; don't flash a loader.
  • Optimistic updates for chat: the message appears at once with "Sending…", then either settles or shows a retry link.
  • Undo over confirm for reversible actions (leave an event, suspend a member); confirm dialogs only for what can't be undone.

States

The life of an action: idle → busy (button disabled, fields read-only) → success (toast or success panel, focus moves to the result) or failed (inline alert, values kept, button resets). Loading regions go loading → loaded, empty, partial (some panels failed) or error; see Empty and error states.

Responsive behaviour

Skeletons use the same grid as the content they replace, so they reflow the same way. Toasts sit above the tab bar on phones and bottom-right on wider screens. Busy buttons keep their width at every breakpoint so the footer doesn't jump.

Accessibility

  • Loading regions carry aria-busy="true" and aria-live="polite" with a hidden "Loading events" label; skeleton pieces are silent.
  • Success toasts go in the role="status" live region; failures use role="alert".
  • Spinners respect prefers-reduced-motion: the rotation stops and the label carries the meaning.
  • Toasts with Undo stay until dismissed or for at least 10 seconds, and pause on hover and focus (WCAG 2.2.1).
  • After success in a dialog, focus moves to the success panel's first button.

Content

  • Busy labels repeat the verb: "Registering…", "Saving…", "Publishing…", "Signing in…".
  • Success toasts lead with the outcome in past tense or second person: "You're registered", "Draft saved".
  • Undo toasts say what's reversible and for how long: "Your spot goes to the waitlist in 10 minutes."
