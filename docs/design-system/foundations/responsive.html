Responsive

Mobile first: what each component does at each breakpoint.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview

  • Touch
  • Testing

Overview

Every screen works from 320px up and is designed at 360, 768 and 1280px. Breakpoints add space and navigation; they never remove content.

Component behaviour

ComponentBelow the breakpointAt or above
Member navigationBottom tab bar (64px), labels under iconsmd: links in the top bar
Admin navigationHorizontal scroller under the brandlg: 16rem sticky sidebar
DialogBottom sheet, full width, 92dvh maxsm: centred, 32rem
DrawerFull widthsm: 32rem from the right
ToastsFull width above the tab barmd: 24rem, bottom right
TablesScroll inside .table-wrap (min 40rem)Fit
FormsOne columnsm: short related fields in two columns
Page headTitle, then actions belowsm: actions beside the title
MessagesList or thread, one at a time, with a back buttonmd: both side by side
Tabs, chip barsScroll horizontally; never wrap to two rowsFit

Touch and pointer

  • Targets are at least 44 × 44px; small buttons, chips, tabs and links extend their hit area under (pointer: coarse) without changing their look.
  • Nothing relies on hover; hover only adds emphasis.
  • Fixed bottom bars sit above the safe area.

Testing matrix

Screenshot each page and mock at 360×800, 768×1024 and 1280×800 in both themes. Check: no horizontal page scroll, no clipped text, dialogs become sheets, navigation collapses, tables scroll inside their wrapper.
