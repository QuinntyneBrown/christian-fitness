Elevation

Shadow levels, surface hierarchy and z-order.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview
  • Levels
  • Surfaces
  • Z order
  • Tokens

Overview

Surfaces are flat with a 1px ring (--color-border-default); shadows appear only for things that float or respond: hovered cards, menus, toasts and dialogs.

Shadow levels

--shadow-1
--shadow-2
--shadow-3
--shadow-4
LevelUse
1Resting next-event card
2Hovered event cards and rows, menus
3Floating hero card, toasts
4Dialogs and drawers over the scrim

Surfaces

Canvas < surface < raised. In the dark theme surfaces get lighter as they rise (stone 950 → 900 → 850), shadows get darker and stronger, and dialogs use --color-bg-surface-raised so they read as raised without relying on the shadow.

Z-order

Components never set a raw z-index; they use a layer token. Sticky chrome sits under menus, the scrim under dialogs, toasts and tooltips on top.
