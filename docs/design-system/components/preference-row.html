Preference row

Preference rows hold one setting each, with what it means, in titled groups on the Preferences page: switches, a choice, a select or an action.

Overview

Use preference rows to lay out a person's settings: which emails and notifications arrive, who can see them, what suggestions use, and their data. Each section (.pref) has a display title, a one-line summary and its rows (.pref__rows), separated by hairlines. A row is one of:

  • Switch row (.switch.pref__row): the switch with its label and description, at 16px.
  • Block row (.pref__row--block): a legend or label over a radio group, a select (.pref__select) or interest toggles.
  • Split row (.pref__row--split): a title and description on the left, an action on the right: Request download, Delete account (.pref__delete, danger outline).
  • After the rows: an action (.pref__action, "Turn off all email"), a note (.pref__note) or a done message (.pref__done).

.prefs lays the page out: a side navigation of the sections and the sections themselves. On Preferences nothing saves until Save in the save bar, so the switches here wait for it, unlike a lone switch.

Anatomy

  1. Title.pref__title: display italic caps, 30px (--font-size-3xl), an <h2> that names the section.
  2. Summary.pref__sub: muted; says where the section stands: "3 of 4 emails on."
  3. Row.pref__row: 16px padding top and bottom, a hairline --color-border-default rule between rows.
  4. ControlThe row's switch, at the row's end; the label and description wrap beside it.
  5. Action.pref__action: a link button after the rows that acts on the whole section.

Sizes

One size. Switch rows take the switch's 44px minimum plus 16px padding top and bottom; the title is 30px; the select is capped at --layout-conversations-width (320px). Sections are 32px apart (--space-8 padding), the first with none above.

States

A row's states are its control's: the switch's hover and focus are forced here with data-state, checked and disabled are real attributes. The page adds three states of its own, carried by the save bar: unsaved, saving (every control disabled) and saved or failed.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 1024px (--layout-breakpoint-lg) .prefs is one column: the side navigation, then the sections, 40px apart.
  • From 1024px it is two columns: a 200px side navigation and sections up to 720px wide, 64px apart; the navigation sticks under the header (--layout-sticky-offset), and each section scrolls to just below it (scroll-margin-top).
  • Switch labels and descriptions wrap beside the switch at every width. Split rows wrap: on a phone the button drops under its text.

Theming

Rules --color-border-default; summary, descriptions and note --color-fg-muted; done message --color-fg-accent; Delete account outlined in --color-danger-border with --color-danger-fg text. Switch colours are the switch's. All follow the theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each section is a <section aria-labelledby> named by its <h2>, reachable from the side navigation's in-page links. Switch rows are the native APG Switch (checkbox with role="switch") inside its label; block rows are a <fieldset> with a <legend>, or a <label for> and its select.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through the controls, section by section.
SpaceFlips a switch or toggles an interest.
↑ / ↓Chooses within the radio group; changes the select.
EnterFollows a side navigation link to its section; presses an action.

Focus

Each control's own ring: 2px --color-focus-ring at 2px offset around the switch track, the radio and the buttons; the select's accent border and halo.

Labelling

A switch's name is its label and description ("Event alerts, New runs, workouts…"); the section title gives context. The legend names a group of radios or interests. The done message is a role="status" live region, so it is announced when it replaces the button.

Motion

Only the switch's own thumb transition (--duration-fast), instant under reduced motion.

Touch

Switch rows are the whole row: at least 44px plus padding. The small buttons extend to 44 × 44px on coarse pointers; "Turn off all email" is a link button 24px tall (--target-min) with no extension.

Content

  • Section titles are one or two words: "Email", "Notifications", "Privacy", "Suggestions", "Your data".
  • The summary says where things stand, in the member's terms: "3 of 4 emails on.", "Who can see you on Christian Fitness."
  • Labels name the setting, never the state: "Let anyone message me"; the description says what on means, or what off means when that is the surprise: "When off, only people in your groups can start a chat".
  • Recommend gently when a setting matters: "We recommend keeping this on."
  • Data actions say what happens and when: "Removes your profile and messages within 30 days".
