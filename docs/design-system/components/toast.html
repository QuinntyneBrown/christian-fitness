Toast

A toast confirms what just happened, or says it didn't, without moving the person away from what they were doing.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablePolite live regionDark surface in both themes

Overview

Use a toast for the result of an action the person just took: registered, saved, published, left an event. It appears at the bottom of the screen (above the tab bar on phones, bottom right on wider screens), stacks with others and goes away on its own unless it carries an action or an error.

  • Info: a neutral confirmation ("Added to your calendar", "Draft saved").
  • Success: the action worked and something new is waiting ("You're registered" with "Open chat").
  • Warning: worth knowing, nothing failed ("Women's Hoops & Devotion Night is 80% full").
  • Danger: the action failed; the toast stays until dismissed and offers "Try again".

Use a banner for a condition that lasts (offline, maintenance), an alert for a failure inside a form or dialog, and an inline message for a problem tied to one field or section. Never put the only copy of important information in a toast.

Anatomy

  1. Tone icon20px (--size-icon-lg) in --color-toast-accent, or --color-toast-warning for warnings; aria-hidden.
  2. TitleOne short sentence, semibold. It carries the message on its own.
  3. Text (optional)Detail in --color-toast-muted: what changed, what happens next.
  4. Action (optional)One text action in the accent colour: "Open chat", "Undo", "Try again".
  5. DismissSmall icon button named "Dismiss".

Sizes

One size. Width comes from the region: the full width minus 16px margins on phones, --layout-toast-width from 768px. Text wraps; the toast grows in height.

States

A toast has an entering, resting, hover/focus (on its controls) and dismissed state. The dismiss button's hover is forced here with data-state; real pages use :hover and :focus-visible. Examples here have no live role so the page does not announce them; in the app the region provides it.

  • Entering: fades and rises 8px over --duration-base with --ease-enter; no animation under reduced motion.
  • Timing: info, success and warning toasts without an action leave after about 6 seconds. The timer pauses while the pointer is over the region or focus is inside it. Toasts with an action, and every danger toast, stay until dismissed.
  • Dismissed: removed from the DOM; focus, if it was inside, returns to the control that caused the toast.

Responsive behaviour

  • Phones (member app): the region spans the width with 16px margins and sits 16px above the tab bar (--layout-tabbar-height).
  • From 768px: the region moves to the bottom right, 24px from the edges, and is --layout-toast-width wide.
  • Admin console: .toast-region--admin drops the tab-bar offset.
  • Toasts never cover the composer or a dialog footer's primary action; the region sits below dialogs in z-order (--z-toast is above content, below modal overlays).

Theming

The toast reads its own tier of tokens, --color-toast-*, so it can stay a dark, high-contrast surface in the light theme and still separate from the dark theme's page. In dark it lifts to a lighter stone so it reads against --color-bg-canvas. Danger uses --color-toast-danger-bg with white text and --color-toast-danger-muted for detail.

/* Re-skin: override the toast tier, never the .toast rules */
[data-theme="dark"] { --color-toast-bg: var(--color-bg-raised); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Toasts render inside one <section class="toast-region" aria-label="Notifications"> that exists from page load. Info, success and warning toasts go into its role="status" aria-live="polite" container; a danger toast is a sibling with role="alert" so the failure is announced at once. Adding the region with the toast would not be announced, so it is always present and empty.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the action and then Dismiss in DOM order; the toast never takes focus on its own.
EnterActivates the action or Dismiss.
EscWith focus inside a toast, dismisses it.

Focus

Focus is not moved to a toast. Inside it, the ring is --color-toast-accent (white on danger), which keeps 3:1 against the dark surface. When a focused toast leaves, focus returns to the control that triggered it.

Labelling

The title is written to stand alone, because screen readers announce the whole toast text once. Dismiss carries aria-label="Dismiss"; the icon is decorative. An undo toast states the deadline in words ("in 10 minutes").

Motion

Enter animation only (--duration-base, --ease-enter), switched off under prefers-reduced-motion. Auto-dismiss timing is long enough to read twice and pauses on hover and focus (WCAG 2.2.1).

Touch

Dismiss is a 36px icon button that extends to 44 × 44px on coarse pointers. The action is a text link with at least 24px height and the toast's padding around it.

Content

  • Title: what happened, past tense, plain: "You're registered", "Draft saved", "Kevin Brooks suspended".
  • Failures say what wasn't done and that nothing is lost: "We couldn't register you. Your spot isn't saved yet."
  • Detail adds what happens next, not a repeat of the title.
  • One action at most, a verb: "Open chat", "Undo", "Try again", "View in the app".
  • No exclamation marks; no "Success!" or "Error".
