Pagination

Pagination splits long admin lists into pages (25 members to a page) and says where you are in them.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: proposed<nav> of linksMock: admin-members

Overview

Use pagination on admin tables that can grow past one screen: members, events, applications. Each page is a URL (?page=3) so Back and sharing work. On phones the numbers collapse to "Page 2 of 8" between Previous and Next.

  • Member-facing lists (events, messages) load more as you scroll or use a "Show more" button instead; they are windowed, not paged.
  • Show the summary ("Showing 21–40 of 154 members") when people need the total.

Anatomy

  1. Summary.pagination__summary, 14px muted.
  2. Previous / NextIcon links with aria-label; aria-disabled at either end.
  3. Current pageInverse filled pill, aria-current="page".
  4. Gap.pagination__gap, an ellipsis hidden from assistive technology.

Sizes

One size: 48 × 48px pills (--target-comfortable).

States

Pages are links. Current uses aria-current="page"; the unavailable end uses aria-disabled="true" on a link without href.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px, numbered pages hide and "Page 2 of 8" shows between Previous and Next.
  • The row wraps rather than overflowing.

Theming

Reads --color-fg-muted, --color-bg-subtle (hover), --color-bg-inverse and --color-fg-inverse (current), --color-fg-disabled.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<nav aria-label="Pagination"> with an ordered list of links. Page links carry aria-label="Page 3".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through Previous, pages and Next; unavailable ends are skipped because they have no href.
EnterOpens the page and moves focus to the table caption.

Focus

Shared 2px ring around the pill.

Labelling

Icon links are named "Previous page" and "Next page". The gap is aria-hidden.

Motion

Touch

48 × 48px targets, 4px apart.

Content

  • "Showing 21–40 of 154 members": en dash for the range, the plural noun of the list.
  • "Page 2 of 8" on phones.
