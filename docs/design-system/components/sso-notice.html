Sign-in notice

Sign-in notes name the Google account a person came back with: the account they are joining with, or the one that could not be used and why.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: new<div>, role="alert" for refusalsMember and admin sign-in

Overview

After Continue with Google, Google hands back an account. These two blocks show which one, so nobody is signed in, or refused, as the wrong person without seeing it.

  • Account (.sso-account): on Join, after Google, the account the profile will be made with: avatar, email, "Signing in with Google" and a Change link back to the start. It replaces the email and password fields.
  • Notice (.sso-notice): the account Google returned could not be used. On member Sign in it has no profile yet and offers Create my profile or Use another account. On Staff sign in it is the wrong domain or has no console role, with a title, the account and what to do. .sso-notice--spaced adds the 32px gap it needs under the staff lead.

When Google itself fails ("Google sign-in didn't finish"), there is no account to name: use a form alert instead.

Anatomy

  1. Account.sso-notice__account: the 16px Google mark (.google-g--sm) and the email, 14px semibold, cut with an ellipsis when long.
  2. Text.sso-notice__text: 15px, relaxed line height; says what happened and what to do.
  3. Primary action (optional)A small primary button or link: "Create my profile".
  4. Other account (optional).sso-notice__other: a small ghost button in the accent colour, "Use another account", back to Google's chooser.

Sizes

One size each. The account row is 14px text in a 14px × 16px padded box with a 40px avatar; the notice has 20px padding (--space-5). Both fill the width of the form (at most --layout-form-max).

States

Neither block is itself interactive; their states are those of the controls inside, plus the moment a notice appears. The Change link has a real :hover underline (no data-state hook); focus is the shared ring, forced here with data-state.

  • Appears: a notice is rendered with role="alert", tabindex="-1" and focus moved to it, so it is announced and the next Tab reaches its actions or the Google button below.
  • Busy: while Join submits, the account row stays as it is and the fields below turn read-only (join google-submitting).
  • Long email: the address is cut with an ellipsis on one line; screen readers still read all of it.

Responsive behaviour

  • Both fill their form at every width. The account row keeps avatar, email and Change on one line: the body shrinks (min-width: 0) and the email ellipsises; Change never wraps.
  • The notice's actions wrap onto a second line when they do not fit (flex-wrap, 8px gap); at 360px "Create my profile" and "Use another account" still share one line.

Theming

The account row sits on --color-bg-canvas inside the surface form, with an accent avatar (--color-accent/--color-fg-on-accent), muted "Signing in with Google" and an accent Change link. The notice is --color-accent-subtle with an accent title (--color-fg-accent) and default text. The ghost "other account" button takes --color-fg-accent and a surface hover. All follow the theme; the Google mark keeps its own colours.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The account row is static content with a link. A refusal notice is a role="alert" region (APG Alert) that receives focus when the page loads with it. The Google mark is aria-hidden; the email is text.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFrom the notice: to its actions, then to the Google button. In the account row: to Change.
EnterFollows Change or Create my profile.
Enter, SpaceUse another account (a button).

Focus

The notice takes focus on arrival (tabindex="-1"), showing the shared 2px ring at 2px offset around its rounded box. Links and buttons inside use the same ring.

Labelling

Change adds visually hidden context, so it is read as "Change how you sign in". The staff title ("That's not a staff account") is the first thing read; the email that Google returned follows, so the person knows which account was refused. The icon is decorative.

Touch

The notice's buttons are small buttons (36px) that extend to 44 × 44px on coarse pointers. The Change link is 14px text with no extended hit area: it should get the coarse-pointer extension that .text-link has.

Content

  • Always show the account Google returned, in full: "dana.reyes@gmail.com".
  • Say what is wrong in the person's terms, then what to do: "Only @christian.fitness accounts can open the console. Pick your work account instead."
  • Staff titles are short and plain: "That's not a staff account", "No admin access yet".
  • Name the roles as the product does: "Ask an Admin to add you to the team".
  • "Signing in with Google" under the email; "Change" (with hidden "how you sign in").
