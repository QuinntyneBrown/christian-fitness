Dialog

A dialog asks for one short task, like requesting a booking or editing a profile, without leaving the page. On phones it rises as a bottom sheet.

Overview

Dialogs hold a short, focused task that belongs to the page behind it: request a booking from a professional's listing, edit your profile. The page behind is covered by a scrim and made inert; focus moves into the dialog and returns to the trigger on close.

  • Use a dialog for a task of one to four fields, or a confirmation.
  • Use a drawer for longer editing alongside a list (admin event and listing editors).
  • Use a page when the task has many steps or deserves its own URL.
  • In the Angular app every dialog opens through the CDK Dialog service; never a hand-rolled overlay or an inline form.

Anatomy

  1. Title.dialog__title, display face 30px; referenced by aria-labelledby.
  2. Subtitle.dialog__sub, the context (who, what, price); referenced by aria-describedby.
  3. Close.icon-button.dialog__close, labelled "Close".
  4. Body.dialog__body, scrolls when content is taller than the dialog.
  5. Footer.dialog__foot: Cancel then the primary, each .btn--grow.

Sizes

One size: up to --layout-dialog-max (512px) wide, 16px clear of the viewport edges, and no taller than the viewport less 64px. On phones it is full width and up to 92% of the viewport tall.

States

The dialog's own states are open, busy, failed and success; its controls keep their own states. Busy marks the primary with aria-busy and leaves the fields readable.

  • Open: the scrim fades in and the dialog scales from 96% (sheet slides up on phones). Focus moves to the first field.
  • Busy: aria-busy="true" on the primary; Escape and Cancel still close, and the request is abandoned with a toast.
  • Failed: an alert in the body; nothing typed is lost.
  • Success: .dialog__success with role="status" and one Done button.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px: a bottom sheet, full width, top corners rounded, sliding up; the footer stays one row.
  • From 640px: centred, up to 512px wide.
  • The body scrolls; header and footer stay fixed inside the dialog.

Theming

The panel reads --color-bg-surface-raised with --shadow-4; the scrim reads --color-bg-backdrop. Dark theme raises the surface a step above the page through tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Dialog (modal): role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby the title and aria-describedby the subtitle. The page behind is inert. CDK Dialog provides the focus trap and focus return.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift + TabCycles focus within the dialog.
EscCloses the dialog and returns focus to the trigger.
EnterSubmits the form from a single-line field.

Focus

On open, focus goes to the first field (or the primary when there is none). On close it returns to the element that opened the dialog. Close the CDK dialog by its ref before navigating.

Labelling

The title names the dialog; the subtitle describes it. Close is an icon button labelled "Close".

Motion

Scrim fades over --duration-base; the dialog scales over --duration-base and the sheet slides over --duration-slow. Under reduced motion all three are removed.

Touch

Close is 44 × 44px; footer buttons are 48px tall and split the width.

Content

  • Title is the task as a verb phrase: "Request a booking", "Edit profile".
  • Subtitle gives the context people need to be sure: who, what, how much.
  • The primary repeats the title's verb: "Send request", "Save changes". Cancel is always "Cancel".
  • Reassure where money or commitment is involved: "Nothing is charged until you both confirm a time."
