Notification row

A notification row is one entry of the member's notifications: what happened, a line of detail, when, and whether it is unread.

Overview

The Notifications screen lists the member's latest 50 notifications from the last 30 days, newest first, as notification rows in a list card (.notes) under a head (.notes-head) with Mark all as read and Notification settings (ADR 0068, ADR 0083). Each row links to what it is about: the event, the series or the host.

  • Read (.note): icon, title, text and time.
  • Unread (.note--unread): a bold title, the unread dot and the visually hidden word "Unread".

The icon says the kind: a clock for a reminder, a crossed calendar for a cancelled event or series date, a calendar with a plus for new dates from a followed host.

Anatomy

  1. Kind icon.note__icon: a 40px accent-subtle circle, aria-hidden.
  2. Title.note__title: medium weight, bold while unread.
  3. Text.note__text: 14px muted, plain text.
  4. Time.note__time: 14px muted: "2 hours ago", "Yesterday", "Apr 12".
  5. Unread markThe unread dot, aria-hidden, and "Unread" in visually hidden text.

Sizes

One size: rows 16px padded, at least 44px tall; the list is at most 48rem wide.

States

Rows are links: hover is forced with data-state="hover", focus with data-state="focus". Unread is a content state.

Responsive behaviour

Full width on phones; the head's actions wrap under the title below 640px. The text wraps; nothing truncates.

Theming

Rows sit in a list card; hover fills --color-bg-subtle. The icon is --color-fg-accent on --color-accent-subtle; text and time --color-fg-muted; the dot --color-accent.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <ul> of links. A row reads its title, text and time, then "Unread" when it is, so weight and the dot are not the only carriers.

Content

  • Titles lead with what changed: "Tomorrow: …", "Cancelled: …", "Cancelled date: …", "Lakeshore Runners posted a new date".
  • The text gives the when and where, or what it means for the member.
  • Times are relative within a day ("5 min ago", "2 hours ago"), then "Yesterday", then the date ("Apr 12").
  • The foot reads "That’s everything from the last 30 days."
