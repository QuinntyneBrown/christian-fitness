Masthead

The masthead heads every public news page: the nameplate, the way in (Sign in, Join free) and, on the front page, the sections.

Overview

The masthead replaced the landing page's site header when the front page became a news page. It is the header of every public page that is not a sign-in form: the news front page, stories, Subscribe and the two legal pages. Members inside the app see the app header instead; the newsletter's web version has its own brand band (Newsletter issue).

  • News (.masthead + .section-nav): the front page. The bar holds the lockup, a ghost "Sign in" and a primary "Join free"; below it, the sections Latest, Events, Training, Nutrition, Faith & Body, Community and Pros. Sticky.
  • Story (.masthead, no section nav): a story page. Same bar, so a reader who arrives from a shared link can still join; the "All stories" back link in the story takes them to the front page.
  • Plain (.masthead--plain): Subscribe. Not sticky, and the actions give way to one quiet "Back to news" (.masthead__back): this page has one job and should not invite a detour to sign in.
  • Legal (.masthead__back--ink): Privacy and Terms. Sticky, with a link to the other legal page in ink, so the two read as a pair.

Anatomy

  1. Bar.masthead__bar: the lockup and actions in one 64px row (--layout-topbar-height), centred in --layout-container-max.
  2. Sign inGhost button, .site-header__signin; hidden below 640px (sign-in stays one tap away from Join).
  3. Join freeThe one primary action on public pages.
  4. Section nav.section-nav: a row of links that scrolls sideways on phones, labelled "Sections".
  5. Current sectionaria-current="page": accent text and a 2px accent underline.

Sizes

One size. The bar is --layout-topbar-height (64px); the section nav adds a row of about 38px links (about 48px on coarse pointers). The whole masthead is about 104px tall on the front page and 64px elsewhere.

States

The bar has no states; its links and buttons do. A section link is current through aria-current="page", which assistive technology announces; hover is forced here with data-state="hover" and focus with data-state="focus". Buttons follow Button.

  • Current is colour plus a 2px underline, so it never relies on colour alone.
  • Section links never disable. A section with no stories still opens and says "No stories in Training yet this week."
  • While the front page loads, the masthead is already complete: it is static, so loading, empty and error states only change what is under it.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px: bar padding 16px; "Sign in" hides (.site-header__signin); the section nav scrolls sideways inside itself with its scrollbar hidden, padding 8px.
  • From 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm): bar padding 24px, section nav padding 16px; Sign in shows.
  • From 1152px: the bar and nav stop growing at --layout-container-max and centre; the border and surface run full width.
  • Coarse pointers: section links grow to 12px vertical padding, about 48px tall.
  • .masthead is position: sticky at the top with --z-sticky; --plain scrolls away with the page.

Theming

The masthead reads --color-bg-surface over a hairline --color-border-default. Section links read --color-fg-muted, hover --color-fg-default, current --color-fg-accent with a --color-accent underline. Back to news sets the ghost button's --btn-fg to --color-fg-muted; the ink modifier sets it to --color-fg-default. Dark theme swaps every one through the semantic tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <header> outside <main>, so it is the page's banner landmark, holding a <nav aria-label="Sections"> of plain links (APG navigation without menus). Sections are pages with their own URLs, so they are links with aria-current, not tabs. A skip link comes first.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift + TabLockup, Sign in, Join free, then each section in order. A focused section scrolls into view in the nav.
EnterFollows the link: the section's page, or the button's destination.

Focus

The shared 2px --color-focus-ring at 2px offset on :focus-visible; the lockup uses a 4px offset. The sticky masthead must not cover focus: pages under it set scroll-padding-top, and the legal sections use scroll-margin-top: var(--layout-sticky-offset).

Labelling

The nav is labelled "Sections", distinct from the member app's "Primary". The lockup's text, "Christian Fitness", names its link; the seal is hidden. Back to news has a visible label and a hidden arrow icon.

Motion

Link colour and underline change over --duration-fast; reduced motion zeroes it. The masthead never hides or shrinks on scroll.

Touch

Section links are about 38px tall with a pointer and about 48px on touch (@media (pointer: coarse)); buttons are 44px. On a phone the section row scrolls by swipe, and the hidden scrollbar is acceptable because the last section is visibly cut at the edge.

Content

  • Sections, in this order: Latest, Events, Training, Nutrition, Faith & Body, Community, Pros. One or two words each; "&" is allowed in Faith & Body.
  • Actions: "Sign in" and "Join free", never "Log in" or "Register".
  • The plain variant's link says where it goes: "Back to news". The legal variant names the other page: "Terms of Service" or "Privacy Policy".
