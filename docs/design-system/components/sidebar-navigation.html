Sidebar navigation

The admin sidebar is where staff move between the console's sections, grouped by the job (Publish, Gather, Community), with counts for what needs them.

Overview

The sidebar is the admin console's primary navigation. It holds the brand, four links with icons, counts for work waiting (applications to review, reports to handle) and, at the bottom, the signed-in staff member with Sign out.

  • Use it on every admin console screen. Member screens use the top bar and tab bar.
  • Counts show work that needs a person; leave them off when the count is zero.
  • Filters within a section are tabs in the page, not more sidebar items.
  • Grouped (.admin-nav__group): with ten sections the console groups them by the job: Dashboard alone, then Publish (Stories, Newsletter), Gather (Events, Event series, Community Pick) and Community (Listings, Services, Members, Feedback). A Moderator sees only Dashboard and Members, with no group labels.

Anatomy

  1. BrandLight seal and "Christian Fitness" with the gold "Admin console" eyebrow.
  2. Item.admin-nav__item: 20px icon and label, 15px semibold, --radius-md.
  3. Count.admin-nav__count pill with its own aria-label ("3 applications waiting").
  4. Signed-in user.admin__user: initials, name, role and the Sign out icon button (44 × 44 px); below 1024px only Sign out shows, at the end of the brand row.

  • Hover fills with --color-bg-on-chrome-hover (forced above with data-state="hover").
  • Focus on the chrome uses --color-focus-ring-on-dark.
  • Items never disable; a staff member without access sees the forbidden page.
  • Group labels are not interactive and have no states.

Responsive behaviour

  • From 1024px (--layout-breakpoint-lg): a sticky 256px column, full viewport height, user block at the bottom.
  • Below 1024px: the sidebar becomes a top band; items scroll horizontally with hidden scrollbars within the band, never the page; the name and role hide and Sign out stays at the end of the brand row.
  • Labels never truncate.
  • Group labels show only from 1024px (.admin-nav__group is display: none below): the phone band is a single scrolling row.
  • From 1024px the column is flex again (brand, links, person; restored in db05e2d): .admin-nav takes the free height and scrolls vertically (overflow-y: auto) when ten items and three labels outgrow a short viewport, so Sign out is never pushed off screen.

Theming

The chrome tokens do the work: --color-bg-chrome, --color-fg-on-chrome, --color-fg-on-chrome-muted, --color-bg-on-chrome-hover, --color-bg-chrome-current, --color-bg-chrome-badge. In dark theme the chrome stays dark and gains a hairline right border so it separates from the canvas.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An <aside> holding <nav aria-label="Admin"> of plain links. No disclosure or tree.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift + TabMoves through items, then Sign out.
EnterFollows the link.

Focus

2px --color-focus-ring-on-dark (borealis), which holds 3:1 on the chrome.

Labelling

Each group is an <h2> naming a <ul aria-labelledby> of its links, so a screen reader announces "Publish, list, 2 items" and can jump between groups by heading; the Dashboard's own list is named "Overview" and a Moderator's single list "Areas", with no heading. The name survives where the heading is hidden below 1024px. Counts carry their meaning: "3 applications waiting", "1 report to review". Sign out is an icon link with aria-label="Sign out".

Motion

Background and colour transition over --duration-fast; reduced motion zeroes it.

Touch

Items are 44px tall; in the phone row they sit 4px apart and scroll sideways.

Content

  • Section names are plural nouns: Dashboard, Stories, Events, Listings, Members.
  • Group labels name the job in one word: Publish, Gather, Community.
  • Count labels say what is waiting, in a full phrase for screen readers.
  • The role under the name is the staff title in sentence case ("Community lead").
