Footer

The public footer closes the landing page with who we are, where, and what we stand for.

Overview

The site footer is two short lines at the bottom of the landing page: the copyright with the city, and the brand's three words. It is quiet on purpose; the page above has already made the case.

  • Use it on public pages only. Member and admin screens end with their content; the tab bar fills the bottom on phones.
  • When policy pages exist, add their links as a third item; keep it to one line on desktop.

Anatomy

  1. Legal line© year, name and city, 14px muted.
  2. Brand line"Health · Energy · Abundant life", the seal's ring words.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px the two lines stack; from 640px they sit on one row, spaced apart.
  • Side padding 16px, 24px from 640px.

Theming

Text reads --color-fg-muted on the page surface; dark theme swaps both.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <footer> outside <main>, so it becomes the contentinfo landmark. Only one per page.
