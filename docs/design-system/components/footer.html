Footer

The footer closes a page with who we are and where, and the few links people look for at the bottom: the newsletter, feedback, preferences and the legal pages.

Overview

There are three footers, all quiet, 14px and muted: the page above has already made its case.

  • News footer (.site-footer with .site-footer__links): the news front page. The copyright and city, then Newsletter, RSS, Send feedback, Privacy and Terms.
  • Plain site footer (.site-footer): Subscribe. The copyright and the brand's three words, "Health · Energy · Abundant life"; no links, because the page has one job.
  • Member footer (.app-footer): under every member page that scrolls (Home, Events, Directory, Services, Profile, event and listing pages, Preferences, Send feedback, error pages): the copyright, then Send feedback, Preferences, Privacy and Terms. A page that fills the window (Messages, .app--fill) has none.

The admin console has no footer. Stories and the newsletter's web version end with their own subscribe band and issue footer.

Anatomy

  1. Legal line© year, name and city, 14px muted.
  2. Links.app-footer__links / .site-footer__links: a wrapping row, 20px apart.
  3. RuleThe member footer sits under a hairline --color-border-default and reserves the tab bar's height on phones.

Sizes

One size. The site footer is centred in --layout-container-max with 32px vertical padding; the member footer's inner row has 24px vertical padding. Links are at least 44px tall (--target-comfortable).

States

Footer links are muted and turn --color-fg-default on hover; focus is the shared ring. The hover rule is :hover only (no data-state hook), so it cannot be forced here.

Footers are the same in their page's loading, empty and error states.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px: the legal line and the links stack; links wrap onto more lines (4px between lines, 20px between links). Side padding 16px.
  • From 640px: one row, spaced apart; side padding 24px.
  • Member footer below 768px: bottom padding of --layout-tabbar-height, so the fixed tab bar never covers it; .app__main:has(+ .app-footer) drops its own bottom padding so the space is not doubled. From 768px the padding goes.

Theming

Text and links read --color-fg-muted on the page (site surface or app canvas), hover --color-fg-default; the member footer's rule is --color-border-default. Dark theme swaps them all.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <footer> outside <main>, so it becomes the contentinfo landmark; one per page. Links are plain links in a paragraph; the RSS icon is aria-hidden and the word "RSS" names the link.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through the footer links after the page content (and, in the member app, before the tab bar).
EnterFollows the link.

Focus

The shared 2px --color-focus-ring; the link's 24px min-height gives the ring room.

Labelling

Links are words, not icons; the middle dots in the legal line read naturally.

Content

  • "© 2026 Christian Fitness · Toronto": update the year each January.
  • Link labels are what people search for: "Send feedback", "Preferences", "Privacy", "Terms", "Newsletter", "RSS".
  • Brand line in sentence case with middle dots, matching the seal.
