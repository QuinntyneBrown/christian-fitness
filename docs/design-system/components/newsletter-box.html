Newsletter box

Newsletter call-outs invite readers to The Abundant Weekly or to join, from the front page's rail, the foot of a story and the Subscribe page.

Overview

Four call-outs, one per place, each asking for one thing:

  • Newsletter box (.newsletter-box): in the front page's rail. A 2px ink frame, the newsletter's name, one line, an email field and "Subscribe free", and a quiet "Or follow by RSS". The field posts to Subscribe, where the address is confirmed.
  • Join card (.join-card): the first thing in the rail, on a bordeaux brand panel: "Life to the full.", what the community is, "Join the community" and social proof. It asks people to join, not to subscribe.
  • Story subscribe band (.story-subscribe): the last thing on a story page, on the dark chrome surface: the newsletter's name, one line and "Subscribe free" as a link to Subscribe. No field: a reader who has just finished a story decides on the next page.
  • Subscribe (.subscribe): the Subscribe page's main column: title, lead, the form with optional extras, and the panels that replace the form when it is done.

Use one call-out per view. A story already ends with the band; do not add a newsletter box to it.

Anatomy

  1. TitleThe newsletter's name in Barlow Condensed italic caps, --font-size-2xl.
  2. TextWhat it is and when it comes, muted.
  3. Email fieldA pill input, labelled "Email address" (visually hidden, the title and placeholder carry it visually).
  4. Subscribe freePrimary button, full width in the rail.
  5. RSS linkQuiet text link to the feeds on Subscribe; 24px tall.

Sizes

The box and join card fill the rail (21.25rem from 1024px; full width below). The band and the Subscribe section stretch to --layout-container-max. Subscribe's field and button are --lg (48px); the box uses the default 44px.

States

The Subscribe form carries the full set of states, each with its real attribute: aria-invalid and aria-describedby on a bad address, disabled and aria-busy="true" on the button while sending, readonly on the field. When the form is done a role="status" panel replaces it and takes focus. The rail's box has only the field and button states; it hands everything else to Subscribe.

Responsive behaviour

  • Phones: the box's field and button stack full width; Subscribe's field and button stack (.subscribe__row); the band's text and button stack, left aligned.
  • From 640px: Subscribe's field and button share a row, the field growing; side padding 24px.
  • From 768px: the band is one row, text left and button right.
  • From 1024px: Subscribe becomes two columns, the form (1.1fr) beside the latest issue preview (1fr); the box and join card sit in the 21.25rem rail.

Theming

The box is the page surface framed in --color-fg-default; its text is --color-fg-muted. The join card is --color-bg-brand with --color-fg-on-brand and --color-fg-on-brand-strong; the band is --color-bg-chrome with --color-fg-on-chrome and --color-fg-on-chrome-muted. Both dark panels use the inverse button and --color-focus-ring-on-dark. Subscribe's panels fill --color-accent-subtle with a 2px --color-accent bar.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <form>s with a labelled type="email" input and autocomplete="email". Extras are a <fieldset> of checkboxes with the legend "Also send me". Results are role="status" panels; failures are role="alert" form alerts. The box and band are named regions (aria-labelledby their titles).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabField, Subscribe free, then the extras and RSS link.
EnterIn the field: submits.
SpaceToggles an extra.

Focus

The field shows the accent border and ring; buttons the shared ring. On the brand and chrome panels the ring is --color-focus-ring-on-dark. After submit, focus moves to the alert or result panel; on invalid it stays in the field, which names the error.

Labelling

The box's label is visually hidden but present: a placeholder is never the label. The error is tied to the field with aria-describedby. The address in a result panel is in strong so people can check it.

Motion

The busy spinner rotates; reduced motion stops it and the words "Subscribing…" remain.

Touch

Fields and buttons are 44px (48px on Subscribe). The RSS link is 44px tall (--target-comfortable). Extras are full-row labels.

Content

  • The name is always "The Abundant Weekly"; say when it comes: "Every Saturday, 7 AM".
  • The button says "Subscribe free"; the join card "Join the community". Never "Sign up" for both.
  • Results say what happened and what to do: "Check your inbox", "You're subscribed", "You're unsubscribed".
  • Errors: "That email doesn’t look right. Check for a typo and try again." "Too many sign-ups." "We couldn't subscribe you."
  • Reassure at the point of asking: "One click to unsubscribe, and we never share your address."
