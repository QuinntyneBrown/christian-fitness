Series card

A series is one program over many dates. These blocks list series, introduce one, sign people up for it and show how far along they are.

Overview

An event series (a run club, a bootcamp, a monthly hoops night) shares one sign-up, one group chat and one host across its dates. Its blocks:

  • Series row (.series-row in a .series-list card): the Events page's Series view. Photo, cadence, name, host and area, a session meter, then price or Joined and the next date.
  • Series page head: a wide banner (.banner-image--wide), the eyebrow, title and facts, and the summary paragraph (.series-summary). The dates below are a session list.
  • Series panel: the sticky sign-up card (.series-panel__title "Sign up", plan options, the register state) and, once joined, series progress (.series-progress).
  • Series card (.series-card): on the page of one dated session, which session it is, a link to the series and the next three dates.

Use an event card for a single date (a series date's card adds a series line), and the host card for who runs it.

Anatomy

  1. Image16:9 photo, --radius-lg, decorative (alt=""); 12rem column from 640px.
  2. MetaRepeat icon and cadence: "Every Tuesday · 6:30 PM", accent semibold 14px.
  3. TitleBarlow Condensed --font-size-3xl; a heading so the list can be navigated by heading.
  4. SubHost · area · date range, muted 14px.
  5. Session meterOne bar per date (see Session list), aria-hidden, with a visually hidden "2 of 7 sessions done".
  6. EndPrice ("$64 for all 8", "Free") or a small Joined tag, then "Next Apr 21 →".

One size each. The series row is 16px padded on phones and 20px from 640px; the series card 20px, 24px from 640px. Series progress bars are 8px tall (--space-2); bars in a row are 6px (--space-1-5).

The series row in the dark theme, full width (it needs the page's width to lay out).
  • Hover fills the row with --color-bg-canvas and turns the title accent.
  • Joined, full and next are said in words ("Joined", "Series full", "Next Apr 21"), never by the meter's colours alone.

Responsive behaviour

  • Series row: one column on phones (image, body, then the end row with price left and next date right). From 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm) a 12rem image column, the body and an end column stacked right-aligned.
  • Banner: .banner-image--wide is 16:9 on phones and 21:7 from 640px (the plain banner is 21:8).
  • Series card: the head wraps the button under the title when it does not fit; the three dates stack, then sit in three columns from 640px.
  • Series panel: below the content on phones; from 1024px it is a sticky aside (.sticky-aside, --layout-sticky-offset).

Theming

Rows sit on the card surface (--color-bg-surface) and hover to --color-bg-canvas. Meta and progress read --color-fg-accent; subs and counts --color-fg-muted. The series card is an --color-accent-subtle panel whose dates and button sit on --color-bg-surface (the card sets --btn-bg to it). Meter bars: done --color-accent, next --color-highlight, upcoming --color-border-default.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The series list is a <ul> of whole-row links whose titles are <h2>s. The series card is a <section aria-labelledby> naming the series. The sign-up panel's choices are a radio group in a <fieldset> with a visually hidden legend ("How would you like to come?"); results are announced by role="status" (joined) or role="alert" (failed).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves from series row to series row; in the panel, into the radio group, then to the button.
EnterOpens the series; activates View series or Join the series.
↑ / ↓Moves between the two plan options.

Focus

The shared ring wraps the whole row; plan options draw it around the option (:has(:focus-visible)). After Join, focus stays on the panel and the confirmation is announced.

Labelling

A row reads: cadence, title, host and dates, "2 of 7 sessions done", price or Joined, next date. The meter is aria-hidden; its count is in visually hidden text. "View series" sits in the section named by the series title.

Motion

Background and title colour over --duration-fast; none under reduced motion.

Touch

Rows are full-width targets well over 44px tall. View series is a small button whose target extends to 44 × 44px on coarse pointers; plan options are 72px tall labels.

Content

  • Cadence names the rhythm and time: "Every Tuesday · 6:30 PM", "Last Thursday of the month · 7:00 PM".
  • Sub: host · area · range: "Lakeshore Runners Fellowship · Etobicoke · Apr 7 – May 26".
  • Price says what it covers: "$64 for all 8", "$10 each", "Free".
  • Series card meta: "Session 3 of 8 · Every Tuesday"; dates as "Tue Apr 28" with the session's topic.
  • Panel results: "You're in for every session", "Registered for 2 dates", "We couldn't sign you up. Try again in a moment."
