Host card

Who runs an event, and what a listing runs: the host card links an event to its host's listing, and hosting rows link a listing back to its events.

Overview

Every event and series is hosted by a directory listing (an organization, an instructor, a church ministry). Two blocks make that link both ways:

  • Host card (.host-card): under the sign-up panel on an event or series page. The listing's mark, "Hosted by · Instructor", the name with a verified mark, and an arrow to the listing. It replaced the old "Posted by the Christian Fitness team" line.
  • Hosting (.hosting of .hosting-row): the Hosting section of a listing's profile; one row per series or one-off event, or a sentence (.hosting__empty) when there is nothing.
  • Verified mark (.verified-mark): the accent badge-check after a verified name, wherever the name appears (host card, listing row).

Use an event card to browse events and a series row to browse series; hosting rows are for one listing's own events.

Anatomy

  1. MarkListing mark 48px: initials on a dark square for organizations, a round photo or blush initials for people. Decorative.
  2. Label"Hosted by", plus the listing's kind on event pages ("· Instructor"); 14px muted.
  3. NameSemibold, one line with ellipsis; accent on hover.
  4. Verified mark.verified-mark: badge-check icon, accent, role="img" aria-label="Verified"; left out when the listing is not verified.
  5. ArrowMuted arrow-right, decorative: the whole card is the link.

Sizes

One size each. The host card is 16px padded with a 48px mark (--size-avatar-xl). Hosting rows are 12px padded with a 5.5rem 4:3 image, 8rem from 640px.

States

Both are links. Hover is forced with data-state="hover" (host card: surface fill; hosting row: lifted shadow), focus with data-state="focus". Neither has an active or disabled state: a listing or event that cannot be opened is not shown.

  • The name's accent hover has no data-state hook on either block, so the matrix shows only the fill and the shadow.
  • Verified is announced ("Verified"), and an unverified listing simply has no mark, never a warning.

Responsive behaviour

  • Host card: fills the aside; the name truncates with an ellipsis and the verified mark never shrinks. Below 1024px the aside, and the card, sit under the content.
  • Hosting row: 5.5rem image column on phones, 8rem from 640px; title and sub truncate to one line each.

Theming

The host card has no fill at rest, a --color-border-default ring, and --color-bg-surface on hover. Hosting rows sit on --color-bg-surface with the same ring and add --shadow-2 on hover. Meta and the verified mark read --color-fg-accent; labels and subs --color-fg-muted.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each is one <a> wrapping the whole card or row, with <span>s inside (no block elements or nested controls). The verified mark is an image role with the name "Verified"; the mark and arrows are aria-hidden. Hosting rows sit in a <ul> under the "Hosting 1" heading.

The shared ring follows the card's --radius-xl and the row's --radius-lg.

The host card reads "Hosted by · Instructor, Danielle Okafor, Verified". A hosting row reads its meta, title and sub in order.

Content

  • Label: "Hosted by" on a series page; "Hosted by · Organization" or "· Instructor" on an event page.
  • Series rows: "Series · Every Tuesday" and "Next: Tue Apr 21, 6:30 PM · 5 sessions left". One-off rows: "Run · Sat Apr 18" and "6:45 AM · Martin Goodman Trail, Marilyn Bell Park".
  • Empty: "Northline Athletic isn't hosting anything right now. Follow to hear when they post a date."
