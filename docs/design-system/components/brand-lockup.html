Brand lockup

The seal and wordmark together are Christian Fitness: a cross inside a ring, beside the name in condensed italic capitals.

Overview

The lockup pairs the seal (a bordeaux cross in a ring) with the wordmark. The seal also appears alone at larger sizes with its ring lettering: "Christian Fitness · Health · Energy · Abundant life".

  • Lockup: top bar and site header, linking home.
  • Light lockup (.lockup--light): on bordeaux or dark chrome; the cross turns gold.
  • Compact lockup (.lockup--compact): the member top bar's. Below 28rem (448px) the seal stands alone in a 44 × 44px link and the wordmark stays in it as visually hidden text (ADR 0084).
  • Seal sizes: lockup (small), sign-in, verse band, hero.

Anatomy

  1. SealInline SVG ring and cross, aria-hidden inside the lockup.
  2. Wordmark.lockup__name, Barlow Condensed italic, uppercase, 20px.

Responsive behaviour

The default and light lockups never shrink or drop their wordmark. The compact lockup, used only by the member top bar, shows the seal alone below 28rem (448px), where the bell, the profile button and Sign out need the room: the wordmark stays in the link as visually hidden text, so the link is still named "Christian Fitness", and the link is 44 × 44px. The bar then fits at 320px without the page scrolling sideways (ADR 0084).

Theming

Ring and wordmark read currentColor (--color-fg-default); the cross reads --color-fg-accent. The light variant uses --color-fg-on-brand and --color-highlight.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

In the lockup the seal is decorative and the wordmark names the link. A standalone seal has role="img" and aria-label="Christian Fitness".
