Component · Containers and overlays

Card

A card is a quiet surface that groups related content: a register panel, a list of professionals, a profile section.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableContainerNot interactive itself

Overview

Cards are white (surface) panels with a hairline inset border and the large --radius-xl corners, sitting on the warm canvas. They hold content; they do not act. When a whole card should be clickable, use the event card or a row link inside a list card.

  • Padded (.card--pad, .card--pad-sm): a panel of related content, like Register on event detail.
  • List (.card--list): a <ul> of rows divided by hairlines, like the Services list.
  • Clip (.card--clip): edge-to-edge media with rounded corners.

Anatomy

  1. Heading or eyebrowOptional first line naming the panel.
  2. ContentText, facts, rows.
  3. ActionsOptional buttons at the bottom.
  4. Surface--color-bg-surface, hairline inset border, --radius-xl.

Sizes

Padding: .card--pad is 24px, 32px from 640px; .card--pad-sm is 24px, 28px from 640px. Width comes from the layout.

States

The card has no states of its own. Rows inside a list card hover and show aria-current for the selected item.

  • Loading cards keep their shape with skeleton lines (.skeleton-stack).
  • A card never disables; its controls do.

Responsive behaviour

  • Cards fill their column; at 1024px detail asides become sticky (.sticky-aside).
  • Padding grows from 640px.

Theming

Reads --color-bg-surface and --color-border-default. In dark theme the surface lifts above the canvas through tokens, and the border carries the edge, not a shadow.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Use the element that fits: <article> with aria-labelledby for a self-contained panel, <ul> for a list card, <div> otherwise. The card adds no role.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to controls inside; the card itself is never focusable.

Focus

Controls inside show the shared ring; rows in a list card show it inset.

Labelling

A list card gets aria-label ("Professionals"). A panel with a heading uses aria-labelledby.

Contrast

Text on card
Muted text on card
Card edge on canvas

Motion

None on the card.

Touch

Rows in list cards are at least 64px tall.

Content

  • Lead with the fact people came for (price, spots left), then the action.
  • Do not title a card "Info" or "Details".
