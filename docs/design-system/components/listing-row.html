Component · Data display

Listing row

The directory lists the churches, clubs, coaches and companies behind every event. A listing row names one and lets people follow it; the listing head opens its profile.

Overview

  • Listing mark (.listing-mark): a listing's picture. Organizations, companies and ministries are initials on a dark rounded square; people (--person) are round, a photo or blush initials. Sizes --sm (40px), default (48px), --xl (112px).
  • Directory groups (.directory-groups of .directory-group): the directory by kind, each with a heading and a one-line blurb; one ungrouped list when a kind chip is chosen.
  • Listing row (.listing-row): a link (mark, name, verified mark, tagline, area, how many events it hosts) and a follow button beside it.
  • Listing head (.listing-head): the top of a listing's profile: big mark, kind and Verified, name, tagline, Follow and Message.

The profile's facts (area, website, followers) are a key facts list; what the listing hosts are hosting rows. Professionals people book are service rows, not listings.

Anatomy

  1. Mark48px listing mark, aria-hidden (the name follows).
  2. Name and verified markSemibold, one line with ellipsis; the verified mark after it.
  3. Tagline and areaOne muted line with ellipsis; below 768px the area follows on its own line (__area--inline). From 768px the area moves to a 9rem column (__area--col) and from 1024px a 7rem hosting column appears (__hosting, accent --some for "1 hosting"); see Variants. They are left out of this figure, which is narrower than the page.
  4. FollowA toggle beside the link, never inside it: "Follow", pressed or not (aria-pressed, check icon when on).

Sizes

Marks: --sm 40px (--size-avatar-md) in mini rows, default 48px (--size-avatar-xl) in rows and host cards, --xl 112px (--size-avatar-3xl) in the head. Initials are 36% of the mark. Rows are 16px padded, 20px from 640px; the head's name is --font-size-4xl, --font-size-6xl from 640px.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768px: mark, name, tagline and the area inline under it; Follow on the right.
  • From 768px (--layout-breakpoint-md): the area moves to its own 9rem column.
  • From 1024px (--layout-breakpoint-lg): the 7rem hosting column appears.
  • Listing head: stacked on phones with Follow and Message sharing the width; from 768px the actions sit right, aligned to the bottom of the name.
  • The kind chips above scroll sideways on phones (chip bar, scroll).

Theming

Organization marks are --color-bg-inverse/--color-fg-inverse (light on dark in the light theme, dark on light in the dark theme); people's --color-accent-subtle/--color-fg-accent. Rows sit on the card surface and hover to --color-bg-canvas. Taglines, areas and blurbs are --color-fg-muted; "1 hosting" and the verified marks --color-fg-accent. The head's rule is --color-border-default.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each group is a <section> with an <h2> and a <ul>. A row holds two siblings: the link to the profile and a toggle button (APG Button, toggle) whose hidden text names the listing ("Follow Christian Fitness"). The head's name is the page's <h1>.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabLink, then its Follow button, then the next row.
EnterOpens the listing.
Space, EnterFollows or unfollows.

Focus

The shared ring wraps the link area (not the Follow button), and the button draws its own pill ring.

Labelling

The row reads: name, Verified, tagline, area (inline area hidden from 768px and the column read instead, never both), hosting. Marks are aria-hidden or decorative images (alt="").

Touch

The link is the full row height (at least 80px). Follow is 36px tall and extends to a 44px target on coarse pointers.

Content

  • Names as the listing writes them ("Rev. James Whitfield", "Faith & Footwork"); taglines are one line about what they do.
  • Kinds and blurbs: "Organizations · Clubs and groups that run events", "Instructors · Certified coaches who lead sessions".
  • Hosting: "1 hosting", "No events yet". Area: a neighbourhood or "Toronto-wide".
  • Head eyebrow: kind, then "· Verified" for verified listings.
