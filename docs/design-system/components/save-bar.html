Component · Containers and overlays

Save bar

The save bar appears when a settings page has unsaved changes, holds the one Save action, and says when the changes are saved or could not be.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newFixed, role="status"Preferences

Overview

Preferences is a long page of switches and selects saved together. Rather than a Save button at the bottom, a dark pill floats above the tab bar the moment something changes: "Unsaved changes · Save". It reports saving, saved (for about two seconds) and failure, then goes.

Use it only for a page of settings saved together. Settings that apply at once use a switch and a toast; forms with a beginning and an end keep their submit button (form layout). A dialog keeps its own footer.

Anatomy

  1. Container.save-bar__inner: a pill (--radius-full) on --color-toast-bg with --shadow-4; 20px start padding, 8px elsewhere.
  2. Text.save-bar__text: semibold, says the state.
  3. ActionA small inverse button: Save, or Try again after a failure; busy while saving.

Variants

Unsaved changes

Unsaved: appears after the first change (preferences/unsaved). The bar is fixed to the viewport; here a frame stands in for the screen.
HTML
<div class="save-bar" role="status"><div class="save-bar__inner"><p class="save-bar__text">Unsaved changes</p><button class="btn btn--inverse btn--sm" type="submit">Save</button></div></div>

Preferences saved

Saved: a check and "Preferences saved", no action; leaves after about 2 seconds (preferences/saved).
HTML
<div class="save-bar" role="status"><div class="save-bar__inner"><p class="save-bar__text save-bar__text--done"><svg class="icon" viewBox="0 0 24 24" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"/></svg>Preferences saved</p></div></div>

Sizes

One size: as wide as its text and button, 52px tall (a 36px button plus 8px padding).

States

The bar's states are the save request's: hidden (nothing changed), unsaved, saving, saved, failed. The button carries the real attributes (aria-busy, disabled); hover and focus on it use data-state.

Both themes

Preferences saved

  • Saving: the settings above are read-only until the answer arrives (preferences/saving).
  • Failed: the region becomes role="alert", the changes stay as set, and Try again re-sends them (preferences/failed).
  • Turn off all email also raises the bar: it is a change like any other, not saved at once (preferences/unsubscribed).

Responsive behaviour

  • Fixed and centred, 16px from each side; below 768px it sits 16px above the tab bar (--layout-tabbar-height + --space-4), from 768px 24px from the bottom.
  • Above page content (--z-toast); the wrapper ignores the pointer so only the pill catches taps.
  • Long text wraps inside the pill; keep it to one line.

Theming

The pill borrows the toast's tokens: --color-toast-bg, --color-toast-fg and the saved check in --color-toast-accent; dark on a light page and light on a dark one. The button is .btn--inverse; its focus ring uses --color-focus-ring-on-dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The wrapper is a live region: role="status" (polite) for unsaved, saving and saved, role="alert" for failed. Keep the region in the page while the screen is open and change its text, so each change is announced. The button is a real type="submit" for the preferences form.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches Save after the page's controls (the bar is last in the DOM).
Enter, SpaceSaves; Try again after a failure.

Focus

The ring on Save uses --color-focus-ring-on-dark. Focus stays where it was when the bar appears; after a save it is not moved.

Labelling

The text is the message: "Unsaved changes", "Preferences saved", "Couldn't save. Your changes are still here." The check icon is decorative.

Motion

None in the CSS: the bar appears and leaves without animation.

Touch

Save is a small button whose target extends to 44 × 44px on coarse pointers.

Content

  • Unsaved: "Unsaved changes" + "Save".
  • Busy: "Saving…".
  • Done: "Preferences saved".
  • Failed: "Couldn't save. Your changes are still here." + "Try again".

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • The bar shares the toast's layer (--z-toast) and bottom offset, so a toast shown while changes are unsaved would cover it. Proposed: stack the toast region above the bar, or give the bar its own offset.
  • The mocks render a new region per state and switch its role between status and alert; a live region added with its message is often not announced. Proposed: keep one region from page load (as the toast region does) and change only its content.
