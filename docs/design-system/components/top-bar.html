Top bar

The top bar carries the brand and the main places in the member app, and the sign-in and join actions on the public site.

Overview

There are two top bars. The app header (.app-header) sits on every signed-in member screen: the lockup, five primary links (Home, Events, Directory, Services, Messages), the profile button and a ghost "Sign out". The site header (.site-header) is the public bar: the lockup, a ghost "Sign in" and a primary "Join free". Since the landing page became the news front page, public pages use the masthead, which reuses the site header's actions (.site-header__actions); no mock renders .site-header on its own now.

  • Use the app header on every member screen; it is the desktop half of primary navigation. On phones its links hide and the tab bar takes over.
  • For public pages use the masthead; keep .site-header for a public page without news sections, where the job is only to get someone signed in or joined.
  • The admin console does not use a top bar; it uses the sidebar navigation.
  • Page titles, filters and actions belong in the page head below the bar, never in the bar.

Anatomy

  1. LockupThe brand lockup, linking to Home. Its text is the link's name.
  2. Primary links.app-nav__link pills, 15px semibold, muted until current: Home, Events, Directory, Services, Messages. Hidden below 768px.
  3. Unread count.count-badge on Messages with aria-label="4 unread".
  4. Profile button.profile-button, a 36px bordeaux disc with initials, aria-label="Your profile"; gains a gold ring when Profile is current.
  5. Sign out.app-header__signout, a ghost button at the end of the bar.

Sizes

One size. The app header is 64px tall (--layout-topbar-height); the site header is at least 72px (--layout-landing-header-height) to give the landing page more air. Both centre their content in --layout-container-max (1152px).

States

The bar itself has no states; its links do. Current is aria-current="page", so assistive technology hears it and the pill shows it in colour and fill.

  • Current uses aria-current="page" on the link; the pill turns bordeaux on --color-accent-subtle.
  • Links never disable. A place that is not ready for the person (offline) still opens and explains itself.
  • The profile button has no hover fill; its current state is the gold ring.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768px (--layout-breakpoint-md) the primary links hide; the lockup and profile button stay, and the tab bar carries navigation.
  • Side padding is 16px on phones and 24px from 640px.
  • The site header hides "Sign in" below 640px; "Join free" always shows, and sign-in is linked from the hero.
  • The app header is position: sticky at the top with --z-sticky; content scrolls under it.

Theming

The bar reads --color-bg-surface and a hairline --color-border-default. Links read --color-fg-muted, hover --color-bg-canvas, current --color-accent-subtle and --color-fg-accent. In the dark theme all of these swap through the semantic tokens; nothing in the rules changes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <header> landmark holding a <nav aria-label="Primary"> of plain links (APG navigation, without menus). The mobile tab bar is a second nav labelled "Primary, mobile" so the two never share a name. A skip link comes before the bar.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift + TabMoves through lockup, links, profile button and Sign out in order.
EnterFollows the focused link.

Focus

Every link shows the shared 2px --color-focus-ring at 2px offset on :focus-visible. The sticky bar never hides a focused element below it: pages set scroll-padding-top to the bar's height.

Labelling

The nav is labelled "Primary". The unread count has its own aria-label ("4 unread") so the link reads "Messages 4 unread". The profile button is labelled "Your profile" because its initials alone mean nothing to a screen reader.

Motion

Link fills transition over --duration-fast; reduced motion zeroes it. The bar never hides on scroll.

Touch

Links are 36px tall on desktop only, where pointers are fine. On touch devices the tab bar's 64px targets take over. The profile button extends to 44 × 44px on coarse pointers.

Content

  • Primary links are one word, nouns, in this order: Home, Events, Directory, Services, Messages. Do not add a sixth; Profile is the button and Sign out sits after it.
  • The site header says "Sign in" and "Join free", never "Log in" or "Register".
  • Counts are numerals, capped at "9+".
