Pick art

Pick art is the Community Pick graphic in each platform's size, with a download per file and a caption to copy; Slack also gets a preview of the post.

Overview

Once staff choose an event (see pick list), the right side of Community Pick shows what to post, one platform at a time, switched with an ink segmented control:

  • Pick art (.pick-art): the branded graphic: photo, a brand shade from the bottom, the "Community Pick" label, the title, when and where, and the note. Shapes: --square (1080 × 1080), --story (1080 × 1920), --landscape (1200 × 630). Its type is sized in container units, so it scales with the box and renders the same at any size.
  • Asset (.asset): one graphic with its size label and a PNG download, laid out in .assets (--insta: post and story side by side; --pair: image and caption side by side).
  • Caption (.caption): the text to paste, pre-formatted for the platform, with Copy and a hint about formatting.
  • Slack preview (.slack-preview): how the post will look in a channel, with the unfurl.

The HTML graphic is the preview; the product renders it to the PNG at the size in the label.

Anatomy

  1. Photo and shade__photo covers the box; __shade fades --color-bg-brand up from the bottom.
  2. Label__label: "Community Pick" pill in the inverse brand colours.
  3. Title__title: Barlow Condensed italic caps, 9% of the width.
  4. When__when: date, time and area in --color-fg-on-brand-highlight.
  5. Note__note: the one-line reason it is a pick.

Sizes

The graphic has no fixed size: it fills its column and keeps the platform's aspect ratio (1, 9 / 16, 1200 / 630). Every text size inside is in container query units (cqw), so the label is 3.2%, the title 9% (6.5% landscape) and the when line 3.4% (2.6% landscape) of the width at any size.

States

The graphic, the caption and the preview are static. Their controls are buttons: PNG (.btn--secondary.btn--xs) and Copy (.btn--neutral.btn--xs); the platform switch is links with aria-current. Pointer and focus are forced with data-state.

Before anything is made

The right side shows the pick empty preview; while assets are created the button reads "Creating…" and the event and note are read-only; if creating fails, an inline error sits above the button and nothing on the right changes.

  • The graphic does not change with the theme: it is brand artwork that is posted as an image.
  • Copied: the Copy button reads "Copied" for about 2 seconds and a visually hidden role="status" says "Caption copied" (L2-072 AC7; ADR 0098).

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px every asset stacks full width; the story graphic is then very tall, so it follows the post.
  • From 640px .assets--insta puts the post and the story side by side (1fr and 0.5fr, so their heights roughly match); .assets--pair puts the image beside the caption (0.8fr and 1fr).
  • From 1024px the assets sit in the right column of .split--pick (21.25rem picker on the left).
  • The platform switch scrolls sideways inside itself when it does not fit (.segmented--flush).

Theming

The graphic uses the brand pair in both themes: --color-bg-brand/--color-fg-on-brand, the label --color-bg-brand-inverse/--color-fg-brand-inverse and the when line --color-fg-on-brand-highlight, with --shadow-2. Captions are surface boxes (--color-bg-surface, --color-border-default ring); the Slack link uses --color-info-fg, standing in for Slack's blue.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each graphic is role="img" with an aria-label naming the event and date, so its inner text is not read twice. Each asset is a <figure> whose <figcaption> names the size. The caption is a <pre> so line breaks survive copying. The Slack preview is labelled "How the post will look in Slack".

Content

  • Size labels name the use and the pixels: "Post · 1080 × 1080", "Link image · 1200 × 630".
  • Captions start with "Community Pick:" and the event, then the note, when, where, price and how to register, in each platform's own marks.
  • The note is one line of at most 70 characters, the same on every graphic and caption.
  • File names: community-pick-<event>-<platform>.png.
