Side navigation

Side navigation lists the sections of one long page beside it, so people can jump to Privacy or to section 4 of the terms without scrolling.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: new<nav> of in-page linksFrom 1024px

Overview

Use side navigation on a long, single page split into titled sections: Preferences and the legal pages (Privacy, Terms). Each link jumps to a section on the same page.

  • Plain (.side-nav__link in a <ul>): Preferences.
  • Numbered (.side-nav__link--numbered with .side-nav__n in an <ol>, under a .side-nav__title "Contents"): Privacy and Terms, whose sections are numbered.

It is not app navigation: moving between screens is the top bar, tab bar and admin sidebar. For views of one list use tabs.

Anatomy

  1. Title (optional).side-nav__title: "Contents", 14px muted semibold.
  2. Number.side-nav__n: 20px wide, tabular figures, so titles align.
  3. Link15px muted semibold (regular when numbered), 8px × 12px padding, --radius-sm; hover fills --color-bg-subtle.
  4. List.side-nav__list: sticky at --layout-sticky-offset, 4px between links.

Sizes

One size. Plain links are 8px × 12px padded (about 40px tall); numbered links are tighter, 6px × 8px. The column is 12.5rem on Preferences (.prefs) and 15rem on legal pages (.legal__grid).

States

Links take hover (data-state="hover") and focus. There is no current-section state in the CSS: the mocks do not mark which section is in view (see Sources). There is no disabled link.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 1024px (--layout-breakpoint-lg): .side-nav is display: none. The page is one column and its section headings are the way through; nothing replaces the list.
  • From 1024px: the left column of a two-column grid; the list sticks at --layout-sticky-offset while the sections scroll, and each section has a matching scroll-margin-top so its heading lands below the sticky header.

Theming

Links read --color-fg-muted; hover --color-bg-subtle with --color-fg-default. The title is --color-fg-muted. No component tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <nav> landmark with an aria-label ("Preference sections", "Contents") holding a list of same-page links (href="#privacy"). Numbered pages use <ol> and write the number as text so it matches the section heading ("4. Faith and health information").

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through the links.
EnterJumps to the section; focus moves with it.

Focus

The shared ring around the link's rounded box. Each target section should be focusable (tabindex="-1") so focus follows the jump.

Labelling

The link text is the section heading, word for word. The visible "Contents" title repeats the nav's label.

Contrast

Link
Link on hover
Title

Motion

Background and colour over --duration-fast. The jump itself does not animate; smooth scrolling, if added, must stop under reduced motion.

Touch

The list only shows from 1024px, where a pointer is expected; links are at least 44px tall (--target-comfortable) and full column width.

Content

  • Use the section headings exactly: "Your data", "Who we share it with".
  • Number only when the sections are numbered in the text: "1.", "2.".
  • Title the list "Contents" on legal pages; Preferences needs no title.
