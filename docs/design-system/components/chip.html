Chip

Chips narrow a list or pick interests in one tap. Tags show those interests back, read-only.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable<a>, <button aria-pressed>, <span>

Overview

  • Filter chip (.chip) in a .chip-bar: each is a link to a filtered view; the current one has aria-current="page". Events by type, professionals by kind.
  • Interest toggle (.interest): a <button aria-pressed> in the edit-profile dialog; several can be on.
  • Tag (.tag): a read-only label on the profile.

Use a tab for views of one thing, a checkbox inside a form that submits, and a badge for status.

Anatomy

  1. Current chipInverse fill (--color-bg-inverse), no ring.
  2. ChipSurface pill with a hairline inset ring, 14px semibold, 8px × 16px padding.

Sizes

One size: chips 36px tall (8px padding, 14px label); interests and tags 6px × 14px padding; .tag--sm 4px × 12px. On coarse pointers chips extend to a 44px target.

States

Filter chips are links: current via aria-current="page". Interests are toggles: pressed via aria-pressed. Pointer and focus are forced with data-state.

  • Filter chips have no disabled state: a filter with no results still opens, and shows a no-results state.
  • Interests are disabled together while the dialog saves (edit-profile busy).

Responsive behaviour

  • The bar wraps onto more lines; chips never shrink or truncate.
  • The divider hides below --layout-breakpoint-sm, where the checkbox wraps to its own line.
  • .chip-bar--scroll does not wrap below 640px: it scrolls sideways with its scrollbar hidden, its negative inline margin running the chips to the screen edge; from 640px it wraps like the default bar.

Theming

Chip: --color-bg-surface, ring --color-border-default (hover --color-border-hover), current --color-bg-inverse/--color-fg-inverse. Interest pressed: --color-accent/--color-fg-on-accent. Tag: --color-accent-subtle/--color-fg-accent.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Filter chips are links in a role="group" with an aria-label; the current one has aria-current="page". Interests are toggle buttons (APG Button, toggle) inside a <fieldset> with a legend.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves between chips.
EnterFollows a filter chip.
Space, EnterToggles an interest.

Focus

The shared 2px ring at 2px offset follows the pill shape.

Labelling

The chip text is the name. The group label ("Filter events") or legend ("Into") gives context. Pressed and current are announced from the attributes, and the fill change is backed by the attribute, not colour alone.

Motion

Background, ring and colour over --duration-fast; none under reduced motion.

Touch

Chips are 36px and extend to 44 × 44px on coarse pointers; 8px gaps (--space-2) between.

Content

  • One or two words, sentence case: "Run", "Walking groups".
  • The first filter is "All".
  • Counts appear only where a chip names a group of listings ("Instructors 2" in the directory); event filters have none, the results say how many.
  • Small tag: one word, "Joined". Outline tags: a listing's focus, "Race training".
