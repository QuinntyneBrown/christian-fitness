Story editor

The story editor is a quiet writing surface: a sticky bar with status and actions, the lead image, headline, summary and story, and the publishing panels beside it.

Overview

Staff write and approve stories here. The page is one <form class="editor">: what is written sits on the canvas with no field borders so it reads like the story will, and every setting lives in the side column's panels.

  • Bar (.editor__bar): back to Stories, the story's status (Draft, In review, Scheduled, Published), when it was saved, and the actions for that status. Sticky at the top.
  • Editor note (.editor-note): the writer's note to the editor, shown only while the story is In review.
  • Lead image (.editor__image) with Replace image, or a dropzone when there is none.
  • Text (.editor__text): headline, summary and story as borderless textareas at reading measure.

Use the editor only for long writing that is previewed as it will publish. Short staff edits (an event, a listing) open a drawer or dialog with ordinary fields.

Anatomy

  1. Back link"Stories": returns to the list.
  2. Status and savedA status pill and "Saved 2 min ago" (hidden below 640px).
  3. Editor note.editor-note: surface card, accent icon, title and text.
  4. Lead image16:9 photo with Replace image in its corner, or a dropzone.
  5. Headline.editor__headline: 30px, 36px from 640px, bold, tight.
  6. Summary and story.editor__summary 20px muted; .editor__story 18px under a hairline.

Sizes

One size. The grid is --layout-container-max wide with a 20rem side column from 1024px; the text column is held to --layout-measure. Headline --font-size-3xl, --font-size-4xl from 640px; summary --font-size-xl; story --font-size-lg.

States

The textareas are real form controls: focus shows the shared ring (forced here with data-state="focus"), an empty required headline after Publish is aria-invalid="true" (a 2px danger ring), and while saving they are readonly. The whole editor's states follow.

  • Approved: after Approve & publish the status reads Published and a toast confirms "Approved and published".
  • Error: a story that fails to load is an inline error with Try again and a link back to Stories; the editor is not drawn.
  • "Saved 2 min ago" is the only autosave signal; it reads "Saving…" and then "Not saved" when saving failed.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px the bar wraps, "Saved …" hides and the actions move under the back link; padding is 16px.
  • 640px to 1023px: 32px side padding; the side column follows the text.
  • From 1024px (--layout-breakpoint-lg): two columns (text, 20rem side) and the side column sticks below the bar.
  • The bar is sticky at the top in every width (--z-raised) and keeps the canvas fill so text scrolls under it.

Theming

The writing surface is the canvas (--color-bg-canvas); the bar has a --color-border-default hairline. Placeholders use --color-fg-subtle, the summary --color-fg-muted, the invalid ring --color-danger-fg. The note is a surface card with an inset --color-border-default ring and a --color-fg-accent icon. The Replace image button sits on the photo with a --color-bg-surface fill and --shadow-1.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native form: each textarea has a visually hidden <label> (Headline, Summary, Story) because the type itself says what each is. The page's <h1> is visually hidden ("Edit story", "New story"). The side column is an <aside aria-label="Publishing">. On invalid or failed save, the alert above the story is focused.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabBack link, Preview, the status actions, then the image, headline, summary, story and the panels.
EnterIn a textarea, a new line; on an action, runs it.
Shift + TabBack from the story to the summary and headline.

Focus

Borderless textareas show the shared 2px --color-focus-ring around their --radius-sm box when focused, so it is always clear which part is being edited.

Labelling

The headline's invalid state is announced from aria-invalid and the focused alert names the fix ("Add a headline before publishing"). The status pill is text. The lead photo is decorative (alt=""); Replace image names its job.

Motion

None of its own. The sticky bar and side column do not animate.

Touch

Bar buttons are the small size (.btn--sm) and extend to 44 × 44px on coarse pointers; the textareas are large targets. The dropzone is the full 16:9 box.

Content

  • Placeholders are hints, never labels: "Headline", "One or two sentences that make people want to read it", "Start writing. Begin a paragraph with > to make it a pull quote."
  • Actions name the outcome for the status: "Approve & publish", "Send for review", "Unschedule", "Update". Busy: "Saving…".
  • Saved line: "Saved 2 min ago", "Saved yesterday", "Not saved yet", "Published today, 7:12 AM".
  • Editor note title: "Note from Priya Raman"; the text is the writer's own words.
