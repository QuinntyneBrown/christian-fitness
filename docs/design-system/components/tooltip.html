Tooltip

A tooltip names an icon-only control on hover and focus. It never holds anything people need to act on.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: proposedrole="tooltip"No mock yet

Overview

Tooltips show a short label above an icon button after a brief delay (--delay-tooltip), on hover or keyboard focus. The button's accessible name comes from the tooltip through aria-labelledby, so the two never disagree.

  • Use for icon-only buttons where the icon may not be obvious: send, copy link.
  • Do not put links, buttons or essential instructions in a tooltip; touch users rarely see it. Use a field hint instead.

Anatomy

  1. Label12px medium caption on the inverse surface.
  2. Shortcut (optional).tooltip__key, a keycap outline.

Sizes

One size; wraps at 240px.

States

Hidden until the anchor is hovered or contains focus, then shown after the delay. Forced open here with data-state="open".

  • Hides on Esc without moving focus.
  • Stays open while the pointer moves onto it (WCAG 1.4.13).

Responsive behaviour

  • Above the anchor, centred; flips below near the top edge.
  • On touch, the tooltip shows on long press only; the name is always there for screen readers.

Theming

Reads --color-bg-inverse and --color-fg-inverse, so it inverts in dark theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Tooltip: role="tooltip" referenced by aria-labelledby (as the name) or aria-describedby (as extra).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFocusing the anchor shows the tooltip.
EscHides it.

Focus

Focus stays on the anchor; the tooltip is never focusable.

Labelling

Its text is the button's name; do not also set aria-label.

Contrast

Tooltip text

Motion

Fades over --duration-fast after --delay-tooltip.

Touch

The anchor keeps its own 44 × 44px target.

Content

  • Two or three words, sentence case, no full stop: "Send message".
  • Match the action, not the icon ("Send message", not "Paper plane").
