Event card

The event card is how members discover what's on: a photo, the date, what and where, and how many are going.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableWhole-card <a>Member app

Overview

Use event cards in the events grid, where photos help people choose. Use an event row where space is tight (home's "Coming up") and a table in the admin console.

  • Default: price in the footer ("Free", "$10").
  • Going: a "Going" pill on the image when the member is registered.
  • Few spots left: the footer's end turns accent: "2 spots left"; "Full" when none.

Anatomy

  1. Image16:9 cover photo, decorative (alt="").
  2. Date badgeSee Date badge; hidden from assistive technology.
  3. Going pillSolid bordeaux, shown only when registered.
  4. MetaType and time in 14px accent semibold; the date is repeated in visually hidden text.
  5. TitleBarlow Condensed --font-size-3xl, a heading so the grid can be navigated by heading.
  6. PlacePin icon and venue, one line with ellipsis.
  7. FooterGoing count, then price or spots left, above a hairline.

Sizes

One size; the card fills its grid cell. Body padding is 20px, 24px from --layout-breakpoint-sm.

States

The whole card is one link. Pointer and focus are forced with data-state. There is no disabled card: a full event still opens so people can see details.

  • Hover lifts with --shadow-2 and turns the title accent.

  • Empty and no results swap the grid for an empty state.

Responsive behaviour

  • One column on phones; two columns from --layout-breakpoint-sm (.event-grid).
  • Cards in a row stretch to equal height; the spacer pushes the footer to the bottom.
  • Long titles wrap; the place truncates to one line.

Theming

Surface --color-bg-surface with a hairline --color-border-default ring; hover adds --shadow-2. Meta, spots left and the title hover use --color-fg-accent; the going pill --color-accent/--color-fg-on-accent.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

One <a> wraps the card; its content is the name. The title is an <h2> in the grid so heading navigation lists the events. No nested links or buttons.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves from card to card.
EnterOpens the event.

Focus

The shared ring around the whole card, following --radius-xl.

Labelling

The card reads as: type, date, time, title, place, going, price. The image and badge are hidden; the date is in visually hidden text in the meta line.

Motion

Shadow over --duration-base, title colour over --duration-fast; reduced motion removes both.

Touch

The whole card is the target.

Content

  • Meta: "Run · Sat 6:45 AM".
  • Titles are the event's name, title case allowed for proper names ("Easter Sunrise 5K").
  • Footer: "142 going"; "Free" or "$10"; "2 spots left"; "Full".
