Section heading

Headings give a page and its parts their names: the page head with its title and subtitle, ruled titles for news columns, section heads with a note, and eyebrows above a title.

Overview

These are typographic blocks, not containers: they name what follows. Use the real heading level the outline needs and pick the class for its look.

  • Page head (.page-head): the row at the top of a list page: title (.page-title), .subtitle, and a page action or search, as in the navigation pattern. --wrap keeps title and action on one wrapping row (the admin console). .page-head__more adds one secondary link under the subtitle (Directory: "Suggest a listing"). .subtitle--narrow caps a long subtitle at --layout-measure-narrow.
  • Title after a back link (.page-title--after-back): a page title that follows a back link (Preferences) gets 16px above it.
  • Rule title (.rule-title): a news-column heading over a 2px ink rule (rail "Upcoming events", "Most read"; the newsletter's "On the calendar"). --inline drops the rule when the title sits in a row that draws its own ("Latest issue" with its number).
  • Section head (.section-head): a .section-title with a muted note or link on the same baseline ("Sessions · 3 done · 5 to go").
  • Small section title (.section-title--sm): a section in a side column ("Connected" on a listing).
  • Eyebrow (.eyebrow): a short accent line above a title. --icon leads with an icon ("Running series · 8 sessions"); --sm is 14px for cards and panels (a suggestion's kind).

A heading inside a card is that card's optional first line; these blocks are for the page and its columns.

Anatomy

  1. Page title.display.page-title, --font-size-5xl; the page's h1.
  2. Subtitle.subtitle, muted; --narrow caps it at the narrow measure.
  3. More link.page-head__more: one secondary link, 8px under the subtitle.
  4. Action or searchThe page's one action, aligned to the title's bottom from 640px.

Responsive behaviour

  • Phones: .page-head stacks title block above the action, 16px apart; --wrap stays a row and wraps.
  • From 640px: .page-head is a row, bottom-aligned, space between.
  • Titles wrap; nothing truncates. .section-head keeps title and note on one baseline row; it does not wrap, so keep notes short ("3 done · 5 to go").

Theming

Titles read --color-fg-default; subtitles and section-head notes --color-fg-muted; eyebrows --color-fg-accent. The rule is --color-fg-default, so it stays ink in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Use real heading elements in order: one h1 per page (the page title), h2 for sections and rule titles, h3 below. The classes never set the level. An eyebrow is a <p> before the heading, not part of it, so heading navigation hears the title alone; its icon is aria-hidden. A section labelled by its heading uses aria-labelledby.

Content

  • Page titles are the place's name, one or two words: "Directory", "Preferences", "Manage stories".
  • Subtitles say what the page is for, in one sentence.
  • Rule and section titles are short nouns: "Upcoming events", "Most read", "Sessions".
  • Eyebrows give context, not the title again: "Running series · 8 sessions".
