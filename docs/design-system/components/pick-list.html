Pick list

Pick lists let staff choose what goes into something: stories for an issue, the series or suggestion to work on, the event for a Community Pick.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newAdmin consoleCheckboxes, radios and current links

Overview

Three ways to choose, each with the native control that matches the choice:

  • Pick list (.pick-list of .pick-rows): several of a few with checkboxes, in the newsletter builder (.builder): "Also this week · 3 of 3". When the limit is reached the rest are disabled.
  • Pick cards (.pick-cards of .pick-cards): links in a <nav> that choose which record the page shows; the shown one has aria-current="page". Dark when current for series; --light (a bordeaux ring) for listing suggestions.
  • Event choices (.event-choices of .event-choices): one of a few with radio buttons and a photo, for the Community Pick. Followed by the pick note (.pick-note) and, before anything is made, the pick empty preview (.pick-empty).

Use interest toggles for short labels (the issue's events), a select for one of many (the big story), and a table for comparing records.

Anatomy

  1. CheckboxNative .check__box, aligned to the first line.
  2. Row title.pick-row__title: 16px semibold, snug.
  3. Row meta.pick-row__meta: section and age, 14px muted.
  4. Card title.pick-card__title, with an optional __tag at its end ("Approved").
  5. Card meta.pick-card__meta: cadence and time, or kind and area.
  6. Count.pick-card__count: "31 / 40 joined" in accent (inverse text when current).

Sizes

One size each. Pick rows: 12px × 16px padding. Pick cards: 16px padding; 16rem wide each in the phone scroller. Event choices: 10px padding with a 4rem × 3.5rem photo. The empty preview is at least 26rem tall.

States

Pick rows and event choices are labels around a native input: checked and disabled come from the input. Pick cards are links: hover is forced with data-state="hover", focus with data-state="focus", current with aria-current="page".

Event
  • Hover on pick rows and event choices: event choices darken their ring (--color-border-hover) on real hover only; there is no data-state hook to show it here. Pick rows have no hover style.
  • Disabled event choices (while assets are created) look exactly like enabled ones: .event-choice has no disabled style. See Drift.
  • Read-only: a scheduled issue disables every row; the list stays readable.
  • Loading: pick cards and event choices are replaced by skeleton tiles of the same height.

Responsive behaviour

  • Pick cards: below 1024px a horizontal scroller of 16rem cards (scrollbar hidden; swipe or Tab to move); --stack keeps them stacked at every width. From 1024px they always stack in the 17.5rem or 18.75rem picker column (.split--picker, --picker-wide).
  • Event choices: one column at every width; the title truncates with an ellipsis, the date and area do not.
  • Builder: the issue card is the main column of .split--builder; the send column follows it below 1024px.

Theming

Rows, cards and choices sit on --color-bg-surface with an inset --color-border-default ring, --color-border-hover on hover. Current pick card: --color-bg-inverse/--color-fg-inverse with its count in --color-fg-inverse; light current and checked event choice: a 2px --color-fg-accent ring. Dates and counts are --color-fg-accent; meta --color-fg-muted.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Pick lists are checkboxes in a <fieldset> whose legend counts the limit ("Also this week · 3 of 3"). Event choices are a radio group (APG Radio Group, native). Pick cards are links in a labelled <nav> ("Series", "Suggestions") with aria-current="page", not tabs: each loads a different record.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabInto the list, then from checkbox to checkbox; into the radio group once; from card to card.
SpaceTicks or unticks a pick row.
↑ / ↓Moves between event choices and selects.
EnterOpens a pick card's record.

Focus

Pick rows: the checkbox's own ring. Event choices: the whole card gets the shared ring when its radio has visible focus (:has(:focus-visible)). Pick cards: the shared ring around the card.

Labelling

The whole row or card is the label, so title and meta are read with the control. The event photo is decorative. Pick card tags ("Approved", "Declined") are text. The empty preview's graphic has role="img" and an aria-label naming the event.

Touch

Every row, card and choice is the whole target and at least 56px tall; checkboxes and radios are small but their labels carry the tap.

Content

  • Legends count the limit: "Also this week · 3 of 3".
  • Pick row: the story's headline, then "Section · age" ("Nutrition · 9 hours ago").
  • Series card: name, "Every Tuesday · 6:30 PM", "31 / 40 joined". Suggestion card: name, "Instructor · Mississauga", "Self-submitted · Yesterday".
  • Pick note hint counts characters: "59 of 70 characters". Empty preview: "Five files, four platforms, one click".
