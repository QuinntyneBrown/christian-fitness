Prose

Prose sets long legal text, the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, with a plain-language summary first and numbered sections people can jump to.

Overview

The legal pages are the only long reading in the product. .legal lays them out so a person can know where they stand in thirty seconds and still find the exact clause:

  1. Back link, title, and "Effective April 1, 2026".
  2. The short version (.legal__summary): five plain sentences on a blush panel, and a note that the full text is what applies.
  3. Numbered sections (.legal__section): "1. What we collect", each with short paragraphs, ruled apart.
  4. Questions? (.legal__questions): who to contact, after a 2px ink rule.

From 1024px a side navigation lists the sections on the left. Use .legal for policies and terms only; stories use the story body, and short help text belongs in the page itself.

Anatomy

  1. Summary title"The short version" in display italic caps, accent.
  2. Points.legal__points: an unordered list with accent dots, --font-size-lg.
  3. Summary noteSays the full text governs.
  4. Section heading.legal__heading (h2), bold --font-size-2xl.
  5. Number.legal__n, accent, part of the heading text.

Sizes

One size. The grid is 15rem of contents and at most 45rem of text from 1024px, with a 64px gap. Text is --font-size-lg with relaxed leading.

States

Legal text is static reading. Its links follow Link; the contents' current section follows Side navigation.

Responsive behaviour

  • Phones: one column; the contents list is hidden (.side-nav shows from 1024px) and the summary comes straight after the title. The summary pads 24px.
  • From 640px: summary pads 32px.
  • From 1024px: two columns, contents 15rem and text up to 45rem.
  • Each section sets scroll-margin-top: var(--layout-sticky-offset) so a jump to it lands below the sticky masthead.

Theming

Text is --color-fg-default on the site surface. The summary fills --color-accent-subtle; its title, the dots and the section numbers use --color-fg-accent / --color-accent. Section rules are --color-border-default; the Questions rule is 2px --color-fg-default with muted text.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain document structure: one h1, the summary a <section aria-labelledby> its h2, each clause a <section id> with an h2 whose number is real text (so "3. Who we share it with" is read whole). The contents is a <nav aria-label="Contents"> with an ordered list of in-page links.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabBack link, contents links (1024px+), links in the text.
EnterJumps to the section; focus and scroll move there.

Focus

The shared ring. In-page jumps land 6rem below the top so the sticky masthead never covers the heading.

Labelling

The summary is first in reading order. Section ids are words (#collect, #sensitive) so links can be shared.

Motion

Touch

Content

  • The short version is five sentences or fewer, each a promise in plain words: "We never sell your information, and we don’t run ads."
  • Name sensitive data plainly: "Your church, faith details and health goals…". Say where data lives: "stored in Canada and handled under PIPEDA".
  • Headings are questions answered or plain nouns: "What we collect", "Who we share it with", "Your choices".
  • Always show the effective date and a real contact address.
