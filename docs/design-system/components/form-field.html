Form field

The form field is the wrapper every input wears: a label above, the control, then a hint or an error below.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.field wrapper

Overview

Every text field, textarea and select sits inside .field. Groups of checkboxes and radios use a <fieldset class="field"> with a legend. The wrapper fixes the order and spacing, carries the field component tokens and ties the parts together for assistive technology.

  • Required is the default and is not marked.
  • Optional adds a muted "(optional)" to the label.
  • With hint: guidance before people type.
  • With error: the error replaces the hint and the control gets aria-invalid.
  • Label row (.field-row): label with a trailing link, such as "Forgot password?".

Anatomy

  1. Label.field__label, 14px semibold, for the control's id.
  2. Optional marker.field__optional, regular weight, muted.
  3. ControlAny input; 6px (--space-1-5) below the label.
  4. Hint or error.field__hint or .field__error, 6px below, referenced by aria-describedby.

Sizes

One size. The wrapper adds no width; the control fills the column.

  • Show errors after submit or when leaving a field that was changed, never while people are still typing.
  • Clear the error as soon as the value becomes valid.

Responsive behaviour

Label always above the control at every width; side-by-side labels break at 320px and with zoom. See form layout for columns.

Theming

.field declares --field-border, --field-border-focus, --field-border-invalid, --field-ring, --field-bg and --field-bg-readonly. Override them on a wrapper to re-skin every control inside.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<label for> plus aria-describedby for hint or error plus aria-invalid. Groups use <fieldset> and <legend class="field__legend">.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the control; the label is not a stop.

Focus

Focus sits on the control; see each control's page.

Labelling

Label, hint and error ids are derived from the control id (f-name, f-name-hint, f-name-error) so they never collide. Errors are text, never just a red border (WCAG 3.3.1).

Motion

None; messages appear instantly.

Touch

Tapping the label focuses the control, enlarging the target.

Content

  • Labels: short nouns in sentence case, no colon.
  • Mark optional, not required.
  • Hints: the rule or why we ask ("Only shown on your profile").
  • Errors: what to do, warm and specific ("Pick a date after today, April 15").
