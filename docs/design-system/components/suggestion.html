Suggestion

A suggestion is a listing a member proposed for the directory, with the four checks staff record before it can be approved.

Overview

On Listings, staff pick a suggestion from the light pick cards and work through it here: who it is, what the member said, who suggested it, and four checks. Approve listing stays disabled until all four pass, and says why.

  • Suggestion (.card.card--pad.suggestion): kind eyebrow, name, area and website, the note as a quote, who sent it, the checks, then the decision.
  • Check row (.check-row): a large checkbox with a label and the standard it stands for.
  • Listed cell (.listed-cell): in the Listed tab's table, the listing's mark, name and verified mark on one line.

Use check rows only for a short fixed checklist that gates one action. Settings that apply at once are switches; choices in a form are checkboxes.

Anatomy

  1. Kind.eyebrow--sm: Instructor, Organization, Company.
  2. Name.suggestion__name: 24px bold.
  3. Area and websiteMuted line; the website opens in a new tab and says so.
  4. Note.suggestion__note: the member's words as a <blockquote> with a left rule.
  5. By.suggestion__by: who suggested it and how they know them.
  6. Progress"3 of 4 passed" beside the Checks legend.
  7. Check row.check-row: 28px checkbox, label, standard.
  8. Decision.suggestion__foot: Approve listing, Decline and the hint, or the result.

Sizes

One size. The card has 24px padding, 32px from 640px. Check rows are 12px block padding with a 28px (--space-7) checkbox. The listed cell's mark is --size-avatar-md.

States

Check rows are labels around a native checkbox: checked and disabled come from it; focus is forced with data-state="focus" on the row. The decision follows the checks.

Checks

4 of 4 passed

  • Unchecked: a new suggestion starts with no checks passed (listings unchecked).
  • A disabled check row has no style of its own: the native checkbox greys and the cursor goes back to default. Text stays full strength so a decided suggestion stays readable.
  • Loading, empty ("No suggestions waiting") and error replace the picker and card with skeletons, an empty state or an inline error.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 1024px the suggestion card follows the stacked pick cards; from 1024px it sits beside the 18.75rem picker (.split--picker-wide).
  • The decision row wraps: the hint drops under the buttons on narrow screens.
  • The listed cell keeps the mark and verified mark on the name's line; the table scrolls inside its wrapper.

Theming

The card is a surface card. Rules (note, checks, foot) are --color-border-default; descriptions and hints --color-fg-muted; the checkboxes are drawn by the browser with accent-color: var(--color-accent); the kind eyebrow is --color-fg-accent.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native form. The checks are a <fieldset> with the <legend> "Checks"; each row is a <label> around its checkbox. The disabled Approve listing is joined to its hint with aria-describedby. The decision is announced with role="status".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabWebsite link, each check, Approve listing (when enabled), Decline.
SpaceTicks or unticks a check.
EnterApproves (when all four pass) or declines.

Focus

The checkbox's own ring; the whole row is the target.

Labelling

Each check reads its label and standard ("Two references, At least one from a church or ministry leader"). The verified mark is role="img" with aria-label="Verified"; the listing mark is decorative.

Touch

Check rows are full width and at least 52px tall; the box itself is 28px.

Content

  • Checks are nouns with the standard beneath: "Credentials", "Certification or registration number verified with the issuing body".
  • Progress: "3 of 4 passed". Hint: "Approve unlocks when all four checks pass."
  • Results say what happened and who was told: "Approved. We’ve sent them a link to claim and publish their listing." / "Declined. The person who suggested it has been told."
  • By: "Submitted by the listing themselves" or "Jordan Reid · knows them".

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • .check-row has no disabled style beyond the cursor; the native checkbox greys. Fine for a decided suggestion, but there is no data-state hook for hover (it has no hover style either).
  • The Approve button is disabled with aria-describedby; a disabled button cannot be focused, so keyboard users never reach its description. Proposed: aria-disabled="true" and refuse the submit, as the forms pattern recommends.
