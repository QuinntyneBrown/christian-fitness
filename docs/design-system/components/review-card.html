Review card

The console's side cards: a call-out for the story waiting for review, the newsletter's send card, the editor's settings panels and the summary of a series.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newAdmin console<a>, <section>

Overview

Four related cards hold what a staff member decides about one thing. They sit beside or above the work, never inside a table row.

  • Review card (.review-card): a whole-card link on the dark chrome, above the Stories table, for each story In review. One per story waiting; none when nothing waits.
  • Send card (.send-card): the newsletter builder's dark card that says when the issue goes out and to how many, and holds Schedule issue.
  • Panel (.card.card--pad-sm.panel): a settings card in the story editor's side column: when it publishes, where it sits on the front page, whether it goes in the Weekly. Groups inside are .panel__group, separated by --ruled hairlines.
  • Series summary card (.series-summary-card): the chosen series' photo, name, host, cadence and place, Edit details, and three stats (joined with a meter, price, sessions left).

Use a banner for a notice about the whole page, a card for general grouping, and a stat for numbers on the dashboard. The member app's series panel is the series card.

Anatomy

  1. Label.review-card__label: 14px semibold in --color-highlight: what waits and since when.
  2. Title.review-card__title: the story's headline, 20px bold.
  3. Meta.review-card__meta: writer and section in --color-fg-on-chrome-muted.
  4. Action.review-card__action: a <span> drawn as an inverse button; the whole card is the link, so it is not a second control.

Sizes

One size each; they fill their column. The review card is full width with 20px padding, 24px from 640px. The send card and panels sit in the 20rem side column of .split--builder and .editor__grid. The series summary image is 8rem × 6rem.

States

The review card is a link: focus is forced with data-state="focus" (the shared ring). It has no hover style and no disabled state. The send card's states are its button's. Panels and the summary card are containers; their states are their controls'.

  • Busy: while the issue schedules, the send card's button reads "Scheduling…" with aria-busy (admin newsletter submitting); while a story saves, every panel control is disabled (story editor saving).
  • Scheduled is the send card's locked state: the builder's fields are read-only and the only action is "Unschedule to edit" (.btn--on-brand).
  • The review card disappears when no story is in review; there is no empty review card.
  • In the dark theme the review card's action keeps --color-fg-default from .review-card__action on the white inverse fill, which fails contrast; see Drift.

Responsive behaviour

  • Review card: wraps, so below about 480px the action drops under the text; padding grows from 20px to 24px at 640px.
  • Send card and panels: below 1024px the side column follows the main column (.split--builder, .editor__grid); from 1024px the column is 20rem and sticky (.sticky-aside, .editor__side).
  • Series summary card: wraps; the stats row always takes the full width in three equal columns; Edit details falls under the text on narrow screens.

Theming

Review and send cards use the chrome: --color-bg-chrome with --color-fg-on-chrome, the label in --color-highlight and meta in --color-fg-on-chrome-muted; they stay dark in both themes. Their buttons are inverse (--color-bg-brand-inverse/--color-fg-brand-inverse) and focus on the chrome uses --color-focus-ring-on-dark. Panels and the summary card are ordinary cards on --color-bg-surface; rules are --color-border-default and fact icons --color-fg-accent.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The review card is one <a> whose text is its name; the "Review" pill inside is a <span>, not a nested control. The send card and panels are <section>s in an <aside aria-label> ("Send", "Publishing"); a panel's choices are a <fieldset> with a <legend>. The summary card's stats are a <dl>; the meter is a native <meter> with fallback text "78% full".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReview card: one stop. Panels: each radio group, select and checkbox in turn.
EnterOpens the story in the editor (review card); schedules the issue (send card).
↑ / ↓Moves between "As soon as it’s published" and "Schedule".
SpaceTicks "Include in the Weekly".

Focus

The review card shows the shared ring outside its --radius-xl corners in --color-focus-ring (it sits on the canvas). Buttons inside the send card use --color-focus-ring-on-dark.

Labelling

The review card reads as: "Waiting for your review · Submitted 1 hour ago, Fasting and training: …, Priya Raman · Nutrition, Review". The summary card's Edit details adds the series name in visually hidden text. Date and time in a panel have visually hidden labels because the legend "When" names them.

Motion

None of their own; buttons transition their fill over --duration-fast.

Touch

The review card is one large target. Send card buttons are 44px tall and full width. Panel radios and the checkbox take their whole label as the target.

Content

  • Review label: what is waiting and since when: "Waiting for your review · Submitted 1 hour ago"; the action is one verb: "Review".
  • Send card: "Goes out" then the date in display type ("Sat Apr 18"), then "7:00 AM Eastern · 6,800 people". Scheduled: "Scheduled for" and "Unschedule to edit".
  • Panel notes say what follows from the choice: "Goes to the All stories and Nutrition RSS feeds when published."
  • Series stats are counts in words and numerals: "31 of 40", "Drop-in $10", "5 of 8".

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • Fixed: .review-card__action set --btn-fg: var(--color-fg-default) on the white inverse fill (1.09:1 in dark); the override is removed, so the button keeps --color-fg-brand-inverse.
