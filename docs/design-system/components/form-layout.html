Form layout

Form layout arranges fields into one calm column, pairs short related fields, and puts the error summary and the submit where people expect them.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.field-grid, .form-alert

Overview

Forms are one column of form fields, top to bottom, with the primary button last. Short related fields (date and time, capacity and price) may pair in a .field-grid from 640px. After a failed submit an error summary appears at the top and takes focus. See the forms pattern for validation timing.

  • Stack: auth screens (join, sign in, password).
  • Two-column grid: editor dialogs.
  • Error summary (.form-alert with .form-alert__list): links to each invalid field.
  • Form alert: a single problem that is not a field's fault ("Sign-in paused for 15 minutes").

Anatomy

  1. Error summaryTop of the form; role="alert", tabindex="-1", focused after submit; links jump to fields.
  2. Field stackFields separated by --space-4 to --space-5.
  3. SubmitPrimary button last; block and large on auth screens.

Sizes

Forms are at most about 28rem wide on auth screens and fill the dialog body in editors. Do not stretch single inputs across a wide page.

States

A form moves through default, invalid, submitting, failed and success.

StateWhat changes
DefaultFields empty or prefilled; primary enabled.
InvalidError summary at top, focused; each field shows its error and aria-invalid.
Submitting (busy)Fields readonly; primary aria-busy="true" with spinner and "…ing" label.
FailedFields editable again; a form alert says what went wrong and how to retry; values kept.
SuccessSuccess alert (.form-alert--success) or navigation onward.
Hover, focus, active, disabledBelong to the controls inside; see their pages.

Responsive behaviour

  • .field-grid is one column below 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm) and two from there.
  • Dialog forms become bottom sheets on phones; the footer stays visible.
  • Order fields so the single-column reading order makes sense when the grid collapses.

Theming

The error summary reads --color-danger-bg and --color-danger-fg; info and success alerts use the info and accent tints. All switch with the theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <form> with native submit. On failed submit, focus the summary (role="alert", tabindex="-1", aria-labelledby its title); each link targets a field id (WCAG 3.3.1, 3.3.3).

Keyboard

KeyAction
EnterSubmits from any single-line field.
TabFrom the summary, moves through the error links, then the fields.
Enter on a summary linkMoves focus to that field.

Focus

The summary shows the shared focus ring when focused; links inside it are underlined.

Labelling

The summary is labelled by its title. Success alerts use role="status" rather than alert.

Motion

No animation; focus moves to the summary instantly.

Touch

Summary links are full-line targets; fields are spaced at least 16px apart.

Content

  • Summary titles count and name the goal: "1 thing to fix before listing".
  • Summary items repeat the field errors word for word.
  • Form alerts say what happened and what to do next, with a link when there is one.
