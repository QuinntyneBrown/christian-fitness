Banner

A banner tells everyone on a page about a condition that lasts: offline, delayed, maintenance, an email still to confirm.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableFull width under the headerFive tones

Overview

Use a banner for something true of the whole app for a while, not the result of one action. It sits directly under the top bar, spans the page and stays until the condition ends or the person dismisses it.

  • Info: a task the person can finish ("Confirm your email" with "Resend").
  • Success: a lasting condition just resolved ("Email confirmed").
  • Warning: the app works, with limits ("You're offline. Showing what was saved at 8:42 AM.").
  • Danger: a service is degraded ("Messages are delayed").
  • Brand: planned announcements from the team (scheduled maintenance).

For the result of an action use a toast; for a failure in a form use an alert. Show one banner at a time; the most severe wins.

Anatomy

  1. Icon20px, names the condition (mail, wifi-off, wrench), aria-hidden.
  2. TextA bold lead sentence, then one sentence of detail.
  3. Action (optional).banner__action, an underlined text link: "Resend".
  4. Dismiss (optional)Small icon button; absent while the condition is still true (offline, delayed).

Sizes

One size. The inner row is capped at --layout-container-max so the text lines up with the page content while the colour spans the full width.

States

Tones are rows. A banner is either dismissible or persistent; its dismiss button has the usual hover and focus, forced here with data-state. Examples carry no live role; see Accessibility.

  • Persistent banners (offline, delayed) have no dismiss; they leave on their own when the condition clears, and a success toast or banner may follow.
  • Dismissed banners stay dismissed for the session; a new condition shows a new banner.

Responsive behaviour

  • Phones: 16px side padding, the icon aligns to the first line and text wraps.
  • From 640px: 24px padding and the icon, text, action and dismiss centre on one row.
  • The banner pushes content down; it never overlays the page.

Theming

Each tone reads its status pair (--color-*-bg, --color-*-fg); brand reads --color-bg-brand and --color-fg-on-brand and switches its focus ring to --color-focus-ring-on-dark. The hairline bottom border uses --color-border-default (transparent on brand).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The banner container exists in the page shell; its content changes. Info, success, warning and brand banners use role="status"; danger uses role="alert" because a service the person relies on is failing. A banner already present at page load is read as page content.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the action, then Dismiss, right after the top bar.
EnterActivates the action or Dismiss. After dismiss, focus moves to the main heading.

Focus

Shared 2px ring; on the brand banner it is borealis (--color-focus-ring-on-dark).

Labelling

The bold lead sentence carries the meaning; the icon is decorative. Dismiss is named "Dismiss". Colour is never the only cue: the text names the condition.

Motion

No motion; the banner appears in place.

Touch

Dismiss is 36px and extends to 44 × 44px on coarse pointers. The action link is spaced 12px from Dismiss.

Content

  • Lead sentence in bold names the condition: "You're offline.", "Messages are delayed."
  • Then what still works and what happens next: "Showing what was saved at 8:42 AM. We'll refresh when you're back."
  • Dates and times in full: "Sunday, April 19, 1–3 AM".
  • Action is one verb: "Resend".
