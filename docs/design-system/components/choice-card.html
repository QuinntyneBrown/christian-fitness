Choice card

Choice cards ask for one answer from a few, as tappable cards or chips that are real radio buttons underneath.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newNative <input type="radio"> in a <fieldset>Member forms

Overview

Use choice cards in a form when one short answer, from two to four options, decides what the rest of the form asks: Who are you suggesting? on Suggest a listing (the fields change for "Myself") and What's it about? on Send feedback (the question and the screenshot field change). Use choice chips for one answer from a longer list of short words: What kind of listing?

  • Card (.choice.choice-card): a radio row drawn as a bordered card, with its visible radio, a semibold label, an optional icon (.choice-card__icon) and an optional description. The chosen card fills with the accent tint and a 2px accent ring.
  • Grid (.choice-grid): lays the cards out; --3 gives three columns from 640px, --4 two columns on phones and four from 640px.
  • Chip (.chip.chip--choice): a chip wrapping a visually hidden radio (.chip__input); the chosen chip fills near-black.

For a plain list of radios with longer descriptions (who can see your profile), use the radio group; for a price-bearing choice, the plan option; for several answers, checkboxes.

Anatomy

  1. Radio.choice__input: the native radio, 20px (--size-icon-lg), tinted with accent-color; it stays visible, so the card is clearly one of a set.
  2. Icon (optional).choice-card__icon: 16px, muted; accent when chosen.
  3. Label.choice__label: 15px semibold; with .choice__desc beneath in 14px muted when the options need a line of explanation.
  4. Card.choice-card: --radius-lg, 12px × 16px padding, a hairline --color-border-default ring on --color-bg-surface; chosen: --color-accent-subtle and a 2px --color-accent ring.

Sizes

One size. Cards are at least 44px tall (--target-comfortable) and as wide as their grid column; chips are the 36px chip. The grid gap is 8px (--space-2).

States

Checked, disabled and invalid are real attributes on the radio; the card and chip style themselves from them with :has(). Focus is forced here with data-state on the radio (on the chip for chips, whose radio is hidden); live it is :focus-visible. Neither the card nor the chip block has a hover style of its own, so there is no hover column; the chip keeps the base chip's ring hover.

What kind of listing?
  • Disabled: while the form sends ("Sending…"), every card and chip is disabled; the label turns --color-fg-disabled on cards, the chip drops to --opacity-disabled.
  • Invalid: never in the mocks, since one option is always chosen by default. If a question ever starts empty, mark the radios aria-invalid (a danger outline on the radio) and say what to pick in a field error under the legend.
  • Changing the answer changes the form below at once (Myself hides "Their email"; Something's off adds Screenshot); nothing is lost that was typed in fields both answers share.

Responsive behaviour

  • .choice-grid alone is one column at every width.
  • .choice-grid--3: one column below 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm), three from 640px.
  • .choice-grid--4: two columns below 640px, four from 640px; short labels with icons fit a 360px phone two by two.
  • Card text wraps inside the card; cards in a row stretch to the tallest. Chips wrap in their .cluster.

Theming

Cards: --color-bg-surface with a --color-border-default ring; chosen --color-accent-subtle with a --color-accent ring; icon --color-fg-muted, chosen --color-fg-accent; radio tint accent-color: var(--color-accent). Chips: the chip's surface and ring; chosen --color-bg-inverse/--color-fg-inverse. All follow the theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native radio buttons in a <fieldset> whose <legend> asks the question (APG Radio Group, natively). Each card or chip is the radio's <label>, so the whole card is the target. The chip's radio is visually hidden but stays in the tab order and in the accessibility tree.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves into the group, to the chosen option, and out again (one stop per group).
↑ / ←, ↓ / →Chooses the previous or next option.
SpaceChooses the focused option if none is chosen.

Focus

Cards: the shared 2px ring at 2px offset around the radio itself. Chips: the same ring around the whole chip, drawn from :has(.chip__input:focus-visible).

Labelling

The legend is the question and the group name; the card's label and description are the option's name and description, read together. Icons are decorative. The chosen card is marked by the filled radio and the 2px ring as well as the tint; the chosen chip by its inverse fill (a strong change in lightness, and the radio state is announced).

Motion

None of their own; the chip's colour change uses the chip's --duration-fast transition, instant under reduced motion.

Touch

The whole card is the target, at least 44px tall and a column wide. Chips are 36px and extend to 44 × 44px on coarse pointers like every chip; 8px apart.

Content

  • The legend asks the question in the member's words: "Who are you suggesting?", "What's it about?".
  • Card labels are short answers in sentence case: "Someone I know", "Something's off"; a description, if any, is one plain line: "A friend, coach or group".
  • Chip labels are the names of the kinds, as the directory shows them: "Organization", "Church Ministry".
  • Choose a sensible default ("Someone I know", "An idea") so the question is never left empty.
