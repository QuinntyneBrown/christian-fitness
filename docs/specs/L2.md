# L2 - Detailed Requirements

Viewports: XS < 576 px, SM >= 576 px, MD >= 768 px, LG >= 992 px, XL >= 1200 px.

## L2-001: Landing page content

**Traces to:** L1-001, L1-018

The landing page shall be a news front page: a masthead with the sections, the top stories, the
latest stories, and a rail with the call to join, the next four events, the newsletter sign-up and
the most-read stories, above the John 10:10 verse.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given a visitor who is not signed in, when they open `/`, then the masthead shows "Sign in", "Join free" and the sections Latest, Events, Training, Nutrition, Faith & Body, Community and Pros, with Latest current (`aria-current="page"`).
2. Given published stories, when Latest shows, then the newest story leads with its image, section, title, summary, author and age; the next four show as headlines with section, title, author, age and image; and the rest follow under "The latest", newest first, each with section, age, title, author, image and its summary where it has one; every story's title leads to its page (L2-061).
3. Given a section other than Latest, when the visitor chooses it, then the address becomes `/?section=<name>`, only that section's stories show, newest first, under the section's name as the page heading, and the top stories are not shown; a section the app does not know shows Latest.
4. Given a section with no stories, when it shows, then it reads "No stories in <section> yet this week."; given no stories at all, then Latest reads "No stories yet. Check back soon."
5. Given the page, when the rail shows, then it holds the headline "Life to the full.", "Join the community" leading to Join, "Upcoming events" with the next four published events in date order (date, title, weekday, time and area, each leading to Join), the newsletter box (L2-057 AC1) and "Most read", the five most-read stories of the last 7 days numbered 1 to 5.
6. Given fewer than four published upcoming events, when the rail loads, then it shows only those and no empty rows; given none, then it reads "No events posted yet. Check back soon."
7. Given the stories fail to load, when the page shows, then the story area says "Stories didn't load." with Try again, and the rail and the rest of the page are unaffected; given the events fail to load, then the rail's list says "Upcoming events didn't load." with Try again, and the stories are unaffected.
8. Given a signed-in member, when they open `/`, then they are redirected to Home.
9. Given the footer, when it shows, then it links to the newsletter's latest issue (L2-059), the RSS feeds (L2-060), Send feedback (L2-063), the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service (L2-067).
10. Given a story on the page, when it is rendered, then its title, summary and author are rendered as text, never as HTML, and nothing on the page identifies or reaches a member.

## L2-003: Create a member account

**Traces to:** L1-002

A visitor joins with name, email, password, optional home church and optional interests.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given valid name, email and a password of at least 8 characters, when the visitor submits Join, then an account is created, they are signed in and taken to Home.
2. Given an email already registered (compared case-insensitively), when the visitor submits Join, then no account is created and the email field shows "An account with this email already exists. Sign in instead?" with `aria-invalid="true"`.
3. Given a password shorter than 8 characters, a blank or whitespace-only name or a malformed email, when the visitor submits either Join form, then the form names each invalid field and creates nothing; a blank name shows "Enter your name." in the error summary and beside the Name field.
4. Given the home church is blank, when the visitor submits valid details, then the account is created with no home church.
5. Given the form is submitting, when the visitor views the button, then it reads "Creating your profile…" and is disabled until the response arrives.
6. Given a name longer than 100 characters or a home church longer than 120, when submitted, then the server refuses it with code `field_too_long`.
7. Given Join, when it shows, then "Sign up with Google" comes first, above a divider "or with email" and the email form; pressing it reads "Opening Google…", disables the button and starts Google sign-in (L2-049).
8. Given a visitor back from Google with a verified Google account that has no member account, when Join shows, then it reads "Finish your profile" with the Google account's email shown as "Signing in with Google" and a Change link back to the email form, a Name filled from Google that can be changed, optional home church and interests, and no email or password field; the email form also labels that field "Name".
9. Given "Finish your profile" with a name, when the visitor submits it, then an account is created with the Google account's email and no password, they are signed in and taken to Home; AC4 to AC6 apply to it.
10. Given a visitor back from Google with an account that already has a member account, when they came from Join, then they are signed in to it and taken to Home, and no second account is created.
11. Given Google sign-in does not finish (L2-049 AC5), when Join shows, then a message above the form says "Google sign-up didn't finish" and that nothing was created, and the email form still works.
12. Given Join, either the email form or "Finish your profile", when it shows, then it has a required checkbox "I'm 16 or older", unticked and never pre-ticked; given it is unticked when the visitor submits, then nothing is sent, an error summary takes focus and links to it, and the checkbox is marked `aria-invalid="true"` with "Confirm you're 16 or older to create a profile"; the server refuses a join without the confirmation with code `age_not_confirmed`; no date of birth is asked for or stored.

## L2-004: Member sign-in and sign-out

**Traces to:** L1-002

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given a registered member, when they sign in with the correct email and password, then a secure, HTTP-only, SameSite session cookie is set and they land on Home.
2. Given a wrong password or unknown email, when they sign in, then they see one generic message "Email or password is incorrect" that does not reveal which was wrong; it adds "If you joined with Google, use Continue with Google." for every email alike, and an account that has no password answers exactly as a wrong password does.
3. Given 5 failed sign-ins for one email within 15 minutes, when a sixth is attempted, then it is refused with code `too_many_attempts` and HTTP 429 until the window passes.
4. Given a suspended member, when they sign in with correct credentials, then sign-in is refused with code `account_suspended` and HTTP 403, and a message to contact the team.
5. Given a signed-in member, when they choose Sign out, then the session is ended on the server and they return to the landing page.
6. Given a visitor who is not signed in, when they open any member route, then they are redirected to sign-in and returned to that route after signing in; a return address that is not a path inside the member app opens Home instead. The one exception is an event link `/e/<id>` (L2-072 AC9): a visitor who opens it is taken to Join, not Sign in, and Join keeps the event as its return address, as does the Sign in link on Join.
7. Given Sign in, when it shows, then "Continue with Google" comes first, above a divider "or with email" and the email form with "Forgot password?" beside the Password label; pressing it reads "Opening Google…", disables the button and starts Google sign-in (L2-049).
8. Given a member back from Google with the Google account their member account uses, when sign-in completes, then the session cookie of AC1 is set and they land on Home, or on the route AC6 kept.
9. Given a visitor back from Google with a verified account that has no member account, when Sign in shows, then a notice (`role="alert"`, focused) names that Google account and says there is no Christian Fitness profile on it yet, with "Create my profile", which opens "Finish your profile" (L2-003 AC8), and "Use another account", which opens Google again; nobody is signed in and no account was created.
10. Given a suspended member who chooses their Google account, when sign-in completes, then it is refused as AC4 states.
11. Given Google sign-in does not finish (L2-049 AC5), when Sign in shows, then a message above the form says "Google sign-in didn't finish" and that they are not signed in, and the email form still works.

## L2-006: Edit own profile

**Traces to:** L1-003

Editing opens a dialog or a screen; there are no inline forms.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given a signed-in member, when they choose Edit profile, then a dialog opens with their current values.
2. Given valid changes, when they save, then the profile shows the new values and the dialog closes.
3. Given a bio over 300 characters, more than 5 goals or a goal over 80 characters, when they save, then the server refuses with code `field_too_long` or `too_many_goals` and nothing changes.
4. Given a member, when they choose whether their home church is visible to other members, then the choice defaults to hidden and is respected by L2-005 AC3.
5. Given a request to change another member's profile, when it reaches the API, then it is refused with HTTP 403 and nothing changes.
6. Given the name is cleared, a bio over 300 characters or more than 5 goals in the dialog, when they save, then the dialog names each field to fix, sends nothing and keeps every value typed.
7. Given the save is refused or no answer arrives, when the dialog shows it, then it stays open with a generic message and every value typed, and nothing on the profile changes.

## L2-009: Browse and filter events

**Traces to:** L1-005

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given published upcoming events, when a member opens Events, then they are listed in date order with date, title, time, place, price and going/capacity; a date that belongs to a series (L2-050) is listed like any other event and adds "Session <n> of <total> · <cadence>".
2. Given a category filter (Run, Workout, Basketball, Mobility, Workshop, Retreat), when the member selects one, then the address becomes `/events?category=<name>`, only that category's events show and its chip has `aria-current="page"`; "All" shows every category.
3. Given a filter with no matching events, when applied, then a no-results message with a Clear filter action is shown, and Clear filter shows all events.
4. Given cancelled, draft or past events, when Events loads, then they are not listed.
5. Given more than 20 events, when the member scrolls to the end, then the next page of 20 loads.
6. Given the events are loading, when Events opens, then skeleton cards show; given none are published and upcoming, then an empty state says so and points home; given they cannot be loaded, then an error state offers Try again, which loads them again.
7. Given a next page that cannot be loaded, when the member reaches the end, then the cards stay and "More events didn't load" offers Try again; given 200 events loaded, then no more are asked for and the list ends with a note.
8. Given a malformed cursor, when the events are asked for, then the server refuses with 400 `validation_failed` and `fieldCodes.cursor` = `cursor_invalid`.
9. Given an address with a category, when Events opens or the member goes back to it, then that category's events show with its chip current; given a category the app does not know, then all events show.
10. Given a category the API does not know, when the events are asked for, then the server refuses with 400 `validation_failed` and `fieldCodes.category` = `category_invalid`.
11. Given Events, when it shows, then a view switch offers "By date" and "Series (<count>)" with the current one marked (`aria-current="page"`), "By date" by default; the page's subtitle reads "From the churches, clubs and coaches in our directory. Free unless marked."

## L2-016: Send and receive messages in real time

**Traces to:** L1-007

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given a member in a conversation, when they send a message of 1 to 2,000 characters, then it appears for every participant within 1 second without reloading.
2. Given an empty message or one over 2,000 characters, when sent, then it is refused with code `message_invalid` and the composer keeps the text.
3. Given a member who is not a participant, when they try to read or send in that conversation, then it is refused with code `not_a_participant`.
4. Given message text containing HTML or script, when displayed, then it is rendered as plain text.
5. Given the real-time connection drops, when it returns, then missed messages are fetched and the member sees a "Reconnecting…" status meanwhile.
6. Given a conversation with more than 50 messages, when opened, then the latest 50 load and earlier ones load on scrolling up; the client holds at most 200 in memory.
7. Given a member opens a conversation, when its messages show, then its unread count becomes zero.

## L2-017: Report a message or member

**Traces to:** L1-007

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given a message from another member, when a member reports it with a reason, then the sender is Flagged for staff review and the reporter sees confirmation.
2. Given a member reports the same message twice, when the second report is sent, then no duplicate report is created.
3. Given a member reports a message with or without a reason of at most 500 characters, when the report is sent, then it records who reported which message and when; a longer reason is refused `field_too_long`. The reason is stored for staff and never logged.
4. Given the sender is already Flagged or is Suspended, when a message of theirs is reported, then the report is recorded and their status does not change; a Suspended sender stays Suspended.
5. Given a member's own message, a message in a conversation the member is not part of, or an unknown message, when the member reports it, then it is refused (`cannot_report_own_message` 403, `message_not_found` 404) and nothing is recorded; a repeat report is refused `already_reported` 409.
6. Given a message from another member in a group or direct chat, when the member chooses Report, then a dialog asks for an optional reason and, once the report is recorded, says "Message reported".

## L2-018: Admin sign-in

**Traces to:** L1-008

Staff shall sign in to the admin app only with a Google Workspace account of the team's domain
that has a console role; the admin app holds no staff password and asks for no code of its own.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given Staff sign in, when it shows, then it names the team's domain ("Use your @christian.fitness Google Workspace account."), offers one action, "Sign in with Google", and says that passwords and two-step verification are managed by Google Workspace; there is no email or password field. Pressing the button reads "Opening Google…", disables it and starts Google sign-in (L2-049) limited to the team's domain.
2. Given a verified Google account of the team's domain that has a staff role, when sign-in completes, then a staff session starts and staff land on the Dashboard.
3. Given a Google account outside the team's domain, when sign-in completes, then it is refused with code `wrong_domain` and HTTP 403 and no session, and Staff sign in shows a notice (`role="alert"`, focused) titled "That's not a staff account" that names the account and says only the team's accounts can open the console, with the button reading "Try another Google account". The domain is checked on the server from Google's signed answer, never from what the browser sends.
4. Given a Google account of the team's domain with no staff role, when sign-in completes, then it is refused with code `not_staff` and HTTP 403 and no session, and the notice is titled "No admin access yet", names the account and says an Admin can add it to the team.
5. Given a request to any admin endpoint without a staff session, when it arrives, then it is refused with HTTP 401 or 403 and no data is returned.
6. Given an admin session idle for 30 minutes, when the next request arrives, then the session has expired and the user must sign in again: the request is refused with code `session_expired` and HTTP 401, the session is ended on the server, and the admin app opens Staff sign in with "Your session expired"; every request within 30 minutes of the one before keeps the session open.
7. Given a signed-in staff member, when they choose Sign out, then the staff session is ended on the server and they return to Staff sign in; their Google session is left alone.
8. Given Google sign-in does not finish (L2-049 AC5), when Staff sign in shows, then a message says "Google sign-in didn't finish" and nobody is signed in.
9. Given a staff member whose Workspace account is suspended or deleted, when they next sign in, then Google refuses them and no staff session starts; a staff session lasts at most 12 hours before Google is asked again, so leaving the team ends console access the same day.
10. Given the team's domain and the Google client, when the system is deployed, then they are settings documented with the deployment (L2-041), and the admin app refuses to start sign-in while either is missing.

## L2-021: Admin navigation and layout

**Traces to:** L1-009

**Acceptance Criteria:**

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given the "By date" or "Series" view, when it shows, then a field labelled "Search events" is available.
2. Given text entered in that field, when the search is applied, then only published upcoming events or eligible series in the current view whose titles contain the trimmed text, without regard to case, are shown in the view's usual order; category and the view's registration filter also apply.
3. Given search text with no matches, when results show, then the no-results state offers an action to clear the search and filters; clearing them restores the current view's unfiltered list. Blank or whitespace-only text does not narrow the list.
4. Given the search changes, when results are requested, then results from the previous selection are replaced, not appended; the date view starts pagination again and existing loading and error states apply.
5. Given the search field at any viewport, when the member uses Events, then it is labelled and keyboard accessible without horizontal page scroll.

## L2-076: Filter events by registration

**Traces to:** L1-005

The Events screen shall let a member limit the date view to their own registered events.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given "By date", when the member selects "Only events I'm going to", then only published upcoming events for which that signed-in member has a registration show, including registered series sessions; category and search also apply.
2. Given the filter is selected, when no events match, then a no-results state explains that none match and offers an action to clear the search and filters; clearing them restores the unfiltered date view.
3. Given the filter is cleared, when results show, then eligible events are no longer restricted by registration; the control communicates its checked state to assistive technology and works by keyboard.
4. Given the filter changes, when results are requested, then pagination starts again for the new selection and does not append results from the previous selection. The server uses the authenticated member's identity, never a member id supplied by the client.

## L2-077: Manage bylines

**Traces to:** L1-022

A byline is a name a story runs under, with a short bio and an optional photo. Only Admins manage bylines; no byline is an account.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given an Admin on Bylines (Stories → Bylines, `/admin/stories/bylines`), when it loads, then every byline is listed by name with its short bio, how many stories use it ("Not used yet" when none) and Edit and Remove; "Add byline" opens the dialog.
2. Given a new deployment, when the seed data runs, then it creates the starting bylines (Naomi Clarke, Priya Raman, Marcus Bell, Pastor Ruth Adeyemi and CF Team) once; running it again adds nothing and changes no byline an Admin has edited or removed.
3. Given "Add byline" or Edit, when the dialog shows, then it has a Name, a Short bio of at most 200 characters and an optional Photo (JPEG, PNG or WebP, at most 5 MB), each labelled, and focus starts on the name; given a missing name or bio, then nothing is saved and a summary takes focus naming each.
4. Given a name already used by another byline (compared without regard to case), when it is saved, then it is refused with code `byline_name_taken` and the dialog marks the name.
5. Given a byline is edited, when it is saved, then every story that uses it shows the new name, published stories included, and a toast says it was saved.
6. Given a byline no story uses, when the Admin chooses Remove and confirms, then it is removed, no longer offered in the story editor, and a toast says so.
7. Given a byline that one or more stories use, when the Admin chooses Remove, then the dialog says how many stories use it, offers only Close and removes nothing; given a story takes the byline while the dialog is open, then the API refuses with code `byline_in_use` and the dialog says so. No story ever loses its byline.
8. Given the story editor's Byline field (L2-069 AC1), when it shows, then it lists the bylines in name order; given there are none, then publishing is refused as L2-069 AC7 states.
9. Given a save or removal fails, when the answer arrives, then a message says so and what was typed is kept; given bylines are loading, then skeletons show; given there are none, then an empty state offers "Add byline"; given they cannot be loaded, then an error state offers Try again.
10. Given a Moderator, when they open Bylines or call a bylines endpoint, then "Admins only" shows and the API refuses with `forbidden_role` (L2-068 AC7).
11. Given any byline is added, edited or removed, when it succeeds, then an audit entry records who and which byline (L2-019 AC3); names and bios are rendered as text, never as HTML.
