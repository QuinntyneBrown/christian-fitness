# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Christian Fitness: a Christian community for health in body and spirit in the Greater Toronto
Area, delivered as a member web app and an admin web app.

## L1-001: Public landing
The system shall present the community, its upcoming events and its trusted professionals to
visitors who are not signed in, and invite them to join or sign in.

## L1-002: Member accounts and sign-in
The system shall let a person create a member account with a profile and sign in and out
securely.

## L1-003: Member profile
The system shall let a member view and edit their own profile (name, bio, home church,
interests, goals) and see the events they are registered for.

## L1-004: Member home
The system shall give a signed-in member a home view of their next events, unread messages and
suggested events.

## L1-005: Events
The system shall let members browse, filter and view events and register for or cancel their
registration within each event's capacity.

## L1-006: Services
The system shall let members browse vetted Christian professionals by category and contact them.

## L1-007: Messages
The system shall let members exchange messages in real time in event group chats and in direct
conversations.

## L1-008: Admin sign-in and access
The system shall give staff a separate admin web app that only staff can sign in to and use.

## L1-009: Admin dashboard
The system shall show staff a dashboard of community activity and work awaiting them.

## L1-010: Manage events
The system shall let staff create, edit, publish, cancel and view registrations for events.

## L1-011: Manage services
The system shall let staff review professionals' applications, approve or decline them, and
edit or remove published listings.

## L1-012: Moderate members
The system shall let staff find members, see their status (Active, Flagged, Suspended) and
flag, suspend or reinstate them.

## L1-013: Security and privacy
The system shall protect accounts and personal data, including faith details, against the OWASP
Top 10, and let each person see and change only what they are allowed to.

## L1-014: Performance
The system shall stay fast on low-power phones and slow networks, and stay smooth through long
sessions.

## L1-015: Accessibility and responsive design
The system shall meet WCAG 2.2 AA and work by touch and keyboard at every viewport from
extra small (< 576 px) to extra large (>= 1200 px).

## L1-016: Observability and operations
The system shall emit telemetry that lets operators see its health without recording personal
data, and shall be deployable and migratable by documented commands.
